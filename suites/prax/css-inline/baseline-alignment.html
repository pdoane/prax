CSS Inline Layout 3 · 4 Baseline Alignment

Baseline alignment

each glyph or inline-level box is aligned in the block axis by positioning its alignment baseline to match the corresponding baseline of its parent (which is its alignment context), and then is potentially shifted from that position according to its post-alignment shift. CSS Inline Layout 3 §4

A smaller box on its parent's alphabetic baseline

The spec's example: "Ap" and a Devanagari word in a child with font-size: 75%. The child box is aligned to its parent by matching up their alphabetic baselines. The red line is the parent's alphabetic baseline.

Corresponding baselines

Baseline alignment always matches corresponding baselines: alphabetic to alphabetic, hanging to hanging, mathematical to mathematical, etc. A box's alignment baseline defaults to matching the parent’s dominant-baseline. Each block is 72px PR Baseline Diagnostic, whose H fills from its hanging baseline to its alphabetic one, with dominant-baseline: hanging.
