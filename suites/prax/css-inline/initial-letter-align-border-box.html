CSS Inline Layout 3 · 7.4 Alignment of Initial Letters: the initial-letter-align property, border-box

initial-letter-align: border-box and synthesis

This property specifies the alignment points used to size and position an initial letter. the over and under alignment points of the initial letter are matched to corresponding over and under points of the root inline box.
