CSS 2 · 10.8.1 Leading and half-leading

Leading: backgrounds that bleed

Margins, borders and padding of inline boxes are drawn but take no room in the line box. Each stage's text is 13px on 20px lines.

Into adjoining line boxes, in document order

Although margins, borders, and padding of non-replaced elements do not enter into the line box calculation, they are still rendered around inline boxes. This means that if the height specified by 'line-height' is less than the content height of contained boxes, backgrounds and colors of padding and borders may "bleed" into adjoining line boxes. User agents should render the boxes in document order. This will cause the borders on subsequent lines to paint over the borders and text of previous lines. CSS 2 §10.8.1
