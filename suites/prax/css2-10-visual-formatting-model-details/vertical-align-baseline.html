The vertical-align property: baseline, sub and super

vertical-align takes baseline | sub | super | top | text-top | middle | bottom | text-bottom | <percentage> | <length>. Initial baseline, not inherited; it applies to inline-level and table-cell elements. Each stage's text is 20px on 60px lines, with its baseline dashed in red. Blue squares are empty 16px inline-blocks, whose baseline is their bottom edge.
