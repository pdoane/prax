CSS 2 · 10.8.1 Leading and half-leading

The vertical-align property: the parent's content area

Each stage's text is 20px on 60px lines, with its baseline dashed in red. Blue squares are empty 16px inline-blocks, whose baseline is their bottom edge. The parent's content area is tinted.

text-top, text-bottom and middle

Align the top of the box with the top of the parent's content area (text-top); Align the bottom of the box with the bottom of the parent's content area (text-bottom); Align the vertical midpoint of the box with the baseline of the parent box plus half the x-height of the parent.
