Leading and half-leading

A font has a height A above the baseline and a depth D below it. The leading L is what 'line-height' adds to A + D, half above and half below. Orange is each span's content area, from its 13px font.

Half the leading above, half below

Still for each glyph, determine the leading L to add, where L = 'line-height' - AD. Half the leading is added above A and the other half below D The height of the inline box encloses all glyphs and their half-leading on each side and is thus exactly 'line-height'. CSS 2 §10.8.1
