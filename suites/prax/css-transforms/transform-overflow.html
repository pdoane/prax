The union of before and after

transforms can extend (but do not shrink) the size of the overflow area, which is computed as the union of the bounds of the elements before and after the application of transforms. CSS Overflow counts only the transformed box (CSS Overflow 3 §2.2). A box laid out at 130 to 190, overflowing by 30, is moved inside to 30 to 90. By CSS Transforms, the union is 190 wide, so the stage scrolls 30px and the box shows at 0 to 60. By CSS Overflow, nothing overflows, and the box shows at 30 to 90.
