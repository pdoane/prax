The Z axis, toward the viewer

Three-dimensional transform functions conceptually extend the coordinate space into three dimensions, adding a Z axis perpendicular to the plane of the screen, that increases towards the viewer. The Z of transform-origin

This module extends the rendering model to account for the existence of three-dimensional transform functions, the Z value of transform-origin: its third value is the depth of the point that a transform is applied about.
