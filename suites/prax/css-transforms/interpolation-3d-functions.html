CSS Transforms 2 · 14 Interpolation of primitives and derived transform functions

Interpolating rotate3d() and perspective()

If the normalized vectors are not equal and both rotation angles are non-zero the transform functions get converted into 4x4 matrices first. Otherwise the rotation angle gets interpolated numerically and the rotation vector of the non-zero angle is used. Angles, or matrices; perspective() a matrix

The transform functions matrix(), matrix3d() and perspective() get converted into 4x4 matrices first and interpolated as defined in section Interpolation of Matrices afterwards.
