CSS Pseudo-Elements 4 · 3.4 Area of a Highlight

The area of a highlight

Each box owns the piece of the overlay corresponding to any text or replaced content directly contained by the box. CSS Pseudo-Elements 4 §3.4 Each stage has 20px Arimo on 50px lines, in a custom highlight with a translucent blue background.

At least the em box, at most the line box

For text, the corresponding overlay must cover at least the entire em box and may extend further above/below the em box to the line box edges.

The space between

Spacing between two characters may also be part of the overlay area, in which case it belongs to the innermost element that contains both characters and is selected when both characters are selected.
