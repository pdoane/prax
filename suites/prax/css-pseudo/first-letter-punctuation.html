CSS Pseudo-Elements 4 · 2.2.1 First Letters and Associated Punctuation

::first-letter: the punctuation with the letter

Preceding and following punctuation must also be included as part of the first-letter text in the ::first-letter pseudo-element. CSS Pseudo-Elements 4 §2.2.1 In each stage the first-letter text is white on orange; every character, spaces too, is 10px wide.

Before the letter

All punctuation that precedes the first letter, as well as any intervening typographic space, characters belonging to the Zs Unicode general category other than U+3000 IDEOGRAPHIC SPACE.

After the letter

Any punctuation other than opening punctuation and dashes that follows the first letter, as well as any intervening typographic space, other than U+3000 IDEOGRAPHIC SPACE or a word separator.
