CSS Pseudo-Elements 4 · 3.6.2 Shadows

Shadows of highlighted text

Any text-shadow applying to a highlight pseudo-element is drawn over its corresponding highlight overlay background. Such text shadows also stack over each other (and over any original text-shadow applied to the text and its decorations, which continues to apply).
