CSS Pseudo-Elements 4 · 3.6.1 Backgrounds

Painting a highlight's background

Each highlight pseudo-element draws its background over the corresponding portion of the highlight overlay, painting it immediately below any positioned descendants. CSS Pseudo-Elements 4 §3.6.1 Each stage holds “Highlighted” in 20px Arimo, in a custom highlight drawn white on blue.

Below positioned descendants, over the rest

::selection over ::highlight()

The ::selection overlay is drawn over the ::target-text overlay which is drawn over the ::spelling-error overlay which is drawn over the ::grammar-error overlay which is drawn over the ::highlight overlays. The selection, white on green, is set by a script; Safari's stills show no selection: their window is never the focused one.
