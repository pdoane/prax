CSS Pseudo-Elements 4 · 2.2.2 Finding the First-Letter Text

Finding the first-letter text

Its first-letter text is the first such inline-level content participating in the inline formatting context of its originating element’s first formatted line, if it is not preceded by any other in-flow content (such as images or inline tables) on its line. CSS Pseudo-Elements 4 §2.2.2 In each stage the first-letter text is white on orange; every character is 10px wide.

Something else first

Only on the first line

Any portion of the first-letter text that is wrapped to the next line no longer forms part of the ::first-letter pseudo-element.
