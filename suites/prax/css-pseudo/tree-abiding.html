Tree-abiding pseudo-elements

Tree-abiding pseudo-elements always fit within the box tree. They inherit any inheritable properties from their originating element; non-inheritable properties take their initial values as usual. CSS Pseudo-Elements 4 §4

Inherited from the originating element

Each element is blue italic text on light orange, with a 2px orange border and 10px of padding; its ::before is content: "before " and nothing else.

Initial values, unless inherit asks

Padding and border are not inherited, so the ::before takes their initial values; the keyword inherit still takes them from the originating element.
