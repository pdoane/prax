CSS 2 · 9.10 Text direction: the 'direction' and 'unicode-bidi' properties

The direction property: paragraphs and neutrals

Hebrew letters are strong right-to-left characters; punctuation, spaces and boxes are neutral.

A forced break ends a paragraph

User agents that support bidirectional text must apply the Unicode bidirectional algorithm to every sequence of inline-level boxes uninterrupted by a forced (bidi class B) break or block boundary. This sequence forms the "paragraph" unit in the bidirectional algorithm. CSS 2 §9.10

Atomic inlines, numbers and mirrored glyphs

All other atomic inline-level boxes are treated as neutral characters always. CSS 2 §9.10 Numbers keep their order, and brackets in right-to-left text are mirrored.
