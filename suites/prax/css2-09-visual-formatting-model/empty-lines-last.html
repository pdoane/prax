CSS 2 · 9.4.2 Inline formatting contexts

Inline formatting contexts: the last line

A forced break ends a line; what comes after the last one makes a line of its own, or a line that is zero high. Text is 13px on 20px lines; the guides are 10px apart.

After the last forced break

Line boxes are created as needed to hold inline-level content within an inline formatting context. CSS 2 §9.4.2 Content after a final <br> goes on a last line, which is empty or not by the same rules.
