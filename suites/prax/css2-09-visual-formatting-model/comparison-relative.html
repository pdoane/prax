CSS 2 · 9.8 Comparison of normal flow, floats, and absolute positioning

Comparing positioning schemes: normal flow and relative

The specification's examples, on a paragraph with an outer span, red, holding an inner one, blue, at font-size: 12px; line-height: 200%: 24px lines.

Normal flow

The P element contains all inline content: anonymous inline text and two SPAN elements. Therefore, all of the content will be laid out in an inline formatting context. CSS 2 §9.8.1

Relative positioning

The inline boxes containing the text (distributed over three lines) are shifted as a unit by '-12px' (upwards). The inner contents are themselves offset relative to the outer contents by '12px' (downwards), back to their original position. Note that the content following outer is not affected by the relative positioning of outer. CSS 2 §9.8.2
