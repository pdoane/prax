CSS 2 · 9.4.1 Block formatting contexts

Block formatting contexts

Boxes in the normal flow belong to a formatting context: block-level boxes to a block formatting context, inline-level boxes to an inline one. Every box is 20px tall; the guides are 10px apart.

One after the other

In a block formatting context, boxes are laid out one after the other, vertically, beginning at the top of a containing block. The vertical distance between two sibling boxes is determined by the 'margin' properties. CSS 2 §9.4.1

Left edges touch the containing block

Each box's left outer edge touches the left edge of the containing block (for right-to-left formatting, right edges touch). This is true even in the presence of floats (although a box's line boxes may shrink due to the floats). CSS 2 §9.4.1
