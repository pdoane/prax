CSS 2 · 9.5 Floats

Floats

A float is a box that is shifted to the left or right on the current line. The most interesting characteristic of a float ... is that content may flow along its side. Each float is 60 by 40.

To the edge, and content along its side

A floated box is shifted to the left or right until its outer edge touches the containing block edge or the outer edge of another float. Content flows down the right side of a left-floated box and down the left side of a right-floated box. CSS 2 §9.5

The top of the current line

If there is a line box, the outer top of the floated box is aligned with the top of the current line box. CSS 2 §9.5
