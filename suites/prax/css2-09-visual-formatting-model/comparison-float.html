CSS 2 · 9.8 Comparison of normal flow, floats, and absolute positioning

Comparing positioning schemes: floating a box

The specification's examples, on a paragraph with an outer span, red, holding an inner one, blue, at font-size: 12px; line-height: 200%: 24px lines.

Floating the inner box

Text flows normally up to the inner box, which is pulled out of the flow and floated to the right margin (its 'width' has been assigned explicitly). Line boxes to the left of the float are shortened, and the document's remaining text flows into them. CSS 2 §9.8.3

With a sibling

A sibling after the inner box: cause the inner box to float to the right as before and the document's remaining text to flow into the vacated space. CSS 2 §9.8.3
