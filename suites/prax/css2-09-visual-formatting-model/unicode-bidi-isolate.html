The unicode-bidi property: isolate and plaintext

CSS 2's unicode-bidi takes normal | embed | bidi-override. Hebrew is right-to-left: אבג reads alef, bet, gimel, from the right. The tinted span is the one each figure styles.

isolate, isolate-override and plaintext

unicode-bidi takes normal | embed | isolate | bidi-override | isolate-override | plaintext. With isolate, neither is the content inside the box bidi-affected by the content surrounding the box, nor is the content surrounding the box bidi-affected by the content or specified directionality of the box. plaintext behaves as 'isolate' except that ... the base directionality ... is determined by following the heuristic in rules P2 and P3.
