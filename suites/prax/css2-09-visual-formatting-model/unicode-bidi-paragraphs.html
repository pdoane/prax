The unicode-bidi property: across paragraphs and split boxes

An element's unicode-bidi adds bidi control codes at its start and end. Hebrew is right-to-left; עברית reads "Hebrew", טקסט "text".

Since CSS 2 · CSS Writing Modes 4 §2.4.4

Closed and reopened at a paragraph break

If an inline box is broken around a bidi paragraph boundary (e.g. if split by a block or forced paragraph break), then the HL3 bidi control codes assigned to the end of the box are also added before the interruption and the codes assigned to the start of the box are also added after it.
