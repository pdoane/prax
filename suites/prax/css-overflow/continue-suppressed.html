CSS Overflow 4 · 5.3 Handling of Excess Content, suppressed content

continue: collapse, not scrollable

continue: normal | discard | collapse. The continue property gives authors the ability to truncate/interrupt the content inside a box, either by visually suppressing the remaining content, or by turning the box into a fragmentation container and discarding the content after the fragmentation break. CSS Overflow 4 §5.3

Suppressed, as by clip

Visually suppressed content is treated as content hidden by overflow: clip: it is not painted, and like ink overflow, does not affect layout or otherwise extend the scrollable overflow area. CSS Overflow 4 §5.3
