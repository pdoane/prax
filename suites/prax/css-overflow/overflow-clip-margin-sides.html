CSS Overflow 4 · 3.2 Changing Clipping Bounds: the overflow-clip-margin-* properties

overflow-clip-margin on each side

overflow-clip-margin-top, -right, -bottom, -left, -block-start, -inline-start, -block-end, -inline-end, and the shorthands overflow-clip-margin, -inline and -block. These properties and their shorthands define the overflow clip edge of the box, i.e. precisely how far outside its bounds the box's content is allowed to paint. The shorthand/longhand relationships are as for margin, but note that the shorthands have a restricted syntax. CSS Overflow 4 §3.2

One side at a time

Each box is overflow: clip, 60 by 30 in a 5px grey border, and holds stripes that reach 40px past it on the top, left and right, and further below. Each expands its clip edge 15px on some sides only.
