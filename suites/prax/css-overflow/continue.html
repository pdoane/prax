CSS Overflow 4 · 5.3 Handling of Excess Content: the continue property

continue: normal, collapse, discard

continue: normal | discard | collapse. The continue property gives authors the ability to truncate/interrupt the content inside a box, either by visually suppressing the remaining content, or by turning the box into a fragmentation container and discarding the content after the fragmentation break. After two lines

Each dashed box is 100px wide with max-lines: 2, holds four lines of .pr-measure text, and is followed by an orange box. normal: the excess content is handled according to the usual rules; collapse: content after the clamp point is visually suppressed, and not in the box's height; discard: the box is a fragmentation container, and Content after the first region break is not rendered.
