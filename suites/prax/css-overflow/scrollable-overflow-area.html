The scrollable overflow area: what it unites

The scrollable overflow area of a box is the union of: Its own padding box. All line boxes it directly contains. The border boxes of all boxes for which it is the containing block and whose border boxes are positioned not wholly within its unreachable scrollable overflow region (if any), accounting for transforms.
