Unreachable overflow: past the scroll origin

the area beyond the scroll origin in either axis is considered the unreachable scrollable overflow region: content rendered here is not accessible to the reader. CSS Overflow 3 §2.3 UAs must clip any content in the unreachable scrollable overflow region. §3.3
