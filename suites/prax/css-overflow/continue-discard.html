CSS Overflow 4 · 5.3.2 Discarding Content

continue: discard

Content that is "not rendered" due to continue: discard is discarded, similar to display: none. Note: continue: discard, and thus this subsection, is at risk. CSS Overflow 4 §5.3.2

After a break, forced or not

The box becomes a fragmentation container that captures region breaks; the break might be forced (e.g. imposed by max-lines or by another mechanism, such as the break-before/break-after properties) or unforced (e.g. if the content would otherwise overflow this fragmentation container due to its size constraints). CSS Overflow 4 §5.3 Each dashed box holds four lines of .pr-measure text.

A multi-column container's overflow columns

if the box is a multi-column container, any overflow columns are also not rendered, nor is any subsequent content. CSS Overflow 4 §5.3.2 The box is 120 by 40, two columns filled in turn (column-fill: auto), with six lines.
