CSS Overflow 3 · 2.1 Ink Overflow

Ink overflow: painted, never scrolled to

Ink overflow is the overflow of painting effects defined to not affect layout or otherwise extend the scrollable overflow area, such as box shadows, border images, text decoration, overhanging glyphs (with negative side bearings, or with ascenders/descenders extending outside the em box), outlines, etc. CSS Overflow 3 §2.1

Effects that reach past the box

Each dashed box is 120 by 60 and holds an 80px wide A 20px in from its left and top. The top row is visible, so each effect paints in full; the bottom row is auto, where the effect is cut at the padding edge, and the box has nothing to scroll: each caption reads its scrollWidth and scrollHeight.
