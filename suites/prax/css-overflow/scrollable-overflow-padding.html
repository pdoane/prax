The scrollable overflow area: padding at the end

The area unites Additional padding added to the scrollable overflow rectangle as necessary to enable scroll positions that satisfy the requirements of both place-content: start and place-content: end alignment. CSS Overflow 3 §3.3

At the end of the content, in each direction

Each dashed box has 20px of padding on two opposite sides and holds a bar, blue at its left or top end and orange at the other, longer than the box; a script scrolls it to the bar's end. Scrolled there, the padding shows past the end.
