SVG 2 · 11.7 Auto-wrapped text

Auto-wrapped text

In the case where the content area is defined by the inline-size property, the ‘x’ and ‘y’ attributes corresponding to the first rendered typographic character define the initial current text position. SVG 2 §11.7 Each stage holds "wraps here wrap", inline-size: 100px, in PR Noto Sans Mono 10 a letter.

The first position, and no other

Except when used to determine the initial current text position, all values ‘x’ and ‘y’ are ignored on ‘text’, and ‘tspan’ elements in auto-wrapped text.

No relative shifts or rotation

the ‘x’, ‘y’, ‘dx’, ‘dy’, and ‘rotate’ attributes are ignored for auto-wrapped text §11.2.2
