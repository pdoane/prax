SVG 2 · 11.1.3 Glyph metrics and layout

The dominant baseline

When a different font (or change in font size) is specified in the middle of a run of text, the dominant baseline determines the baseline used to align glyphs in the new font (new size) to those in the previous font. SVG 2 §11.1.3 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 80. The text is PR Baseline Diagnostic Zero at 30px, whose "H" fills the band from its alphabetic baseline up to its hanging baseline, 0.65 em above; the text's y is dashed in red.
