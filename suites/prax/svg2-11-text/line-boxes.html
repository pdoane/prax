SVG 2 · 11.1.3 Glyph metrics and layout

Line boxes

Multi-line text is produced by stacking these boxes. The height of a line box is determined by finding the maximum ascent and the maximum descent of all the glyphs in a line of text after applying the effect of the line-height property. SVG 2 §11.1.3 The text is PR Noto Sans Mono, 10 a letter, with white-space: pre-line; line-height: 20px, so a line feed breaks the line; the expected lines are dashed in red.
