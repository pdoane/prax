SVG 2 · 11.8.2 Attributes

textPath: side

Determines the side of the path the text is placed on (relative to the path direction). Specifying a value of right effectively reverses the path. SVG 2 §11.8.2 The working group kept it in 2026, as svgwg#1068 records. The text is PR Noto Sans Mono at calc(50px / 3), 10 a letter, on paths dashed in red.

A line

A line from 20, 40 to 140, 40, 120 long, its start marked.

A circle

A closed path, a circle of radius 30 at 80, 45, starting at its right and going clockwise.
