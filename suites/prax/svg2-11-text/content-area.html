SVG 2 · 11.4 Text layout – Content Area

The content area

A content area is defined by specifying in a ‘text’ element an inline-size property. If no content area is provided, the content area defaults to a rectangle of infinite width and height. Once a wrapping area is defined, the text is laid out inside the wrapping area according to the rules of CSS. SVG 2 §11.4 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 80. The text is PR Noto Sans Mono at calc(50px / 3) on 20px lines, each letter 10 wide, at x="20" y="30".
