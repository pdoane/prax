SVG 2 · 11.8.3 Text on a path layout rules

Glyphs on a path

Conceptually, for text on a path the target path is stretched out into either a horizontal or vertical straight line segment. SVG 2 §11.8.3 Paths are dashed in red.

Each glyph turned at its midpoint

Position the glyph such that the glyph-midline passes through the midpoint-on-the-path and is perpendicular to the line through the startpoint-on-the-path and the endpoint-on-the-path. "LLLLLL" in 30px PR Noto Sans Mono, 18 a letter, on a path up from 20, 60 to a corner at 80, 20, 72 along, and down to 140, 60.
