SVG 2 · 11.10 Properties and pseudo-elements

Pseudo-elements on text

Additionally, the @font-face rule must be supported for font selection as well as the ::first-line and ::first-letter pseudo-elements must be supported on ‘text’ elements. SVG 2 §11.10 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60 holding text in PR Noto Sans Mono at calc(50px / 3), 10 a letter, at x="20" y="35", filled blue.

::first-letter and ::first-line

No ::before or ::after

Additionally, the ::before and ::after generated content pseudo-elements must not apply to SVG text elements.
