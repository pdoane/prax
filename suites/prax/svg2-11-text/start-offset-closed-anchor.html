SVG 2 · 11.8.2 Attributes

Anchoring on a closed path

For paths consisting of a single closed subpath (including an equivalent path for a basic shape), typographic characters are rendered along one complete circuit of the path. SVG 2 §11.8.2 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 90 holding a closed path, a circle of radius 30 at 80, 45, 188.5 around, dashed in red, that starts at its right and goes clockwise. The text is PR Noto Sans Mono at calc(50px / 3), 10 a letter.

Anchored to the offset

For the middle, the text is rendered from the middle point in both directions until a point on the path equal distance in both directions from the initial position on the path is reached. Here startOffset="0", the path's start at the right, marked solid red.
