SVG 2 · 11.5 Text layout – Algorithm

Characters that layout discards

A character is not addressable if it was discarded during layout due to being a collapsed white space character, a soft hyphen character, or a bidi control character, discarded during layout due to being a collapsed segment break, or trimmed from the start or end of a line.
