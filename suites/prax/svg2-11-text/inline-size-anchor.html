SVG 2 · 11.4.1 The ‘inline-size’ property

inline-size: where the rectangle goes

For left-to-right text, the initial current text position is at the left of the rectangle. For right-to-left text it is at the right of the rectangle. The rectangle (wrapping area) is then anchored according to the text-anchor property using the edges of the wrapping area to determine the start, middle, and end positions.
