SVG 2 · 11.8.3 Text on a path layout rules

After a textPath

After positioning all characters within the ‘textPath’, the current text position is set to the end point of the path, after adjusting for the ‘startOffset’ in the case of paths that are a single closed loop. In other words, text that follows a ‘textPath’ element (but is still inside a ‘text’ element) that does not have explicit positioning information (‘x’ and ‘y’ attributes) is positioned from the end of the path.
