SVG 2 · 11.10.2.3 The ‘writing-mode’ property

writing-mode in SVG text

This property sets the block-flow direction; or in-other-words, the direction in which lines of text are stacked. As a consequence it also determines if the text has a horizontal or vertical orientation. SVG 2 §11.10.2.3 The section is not normative; nothing here is flagged.
