SVG 2 · 11.8.2 Attributes

startOffset on a closed path

For paths consisting of a single closed subpath (including an equivalent path for a basic shape), typographic characters are rendered along one complete circuit of the path. SVG 2 §11.8.2 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 90 holding a closed path, a circle of radius 30 at 80, 45, 188.5 around, dashed in red, that starts at its right and goes clockwise. The text is PR Noto Sans Mono at calc(50px / 3), 10 a letter, with startOffset="75%": at the top, marked solid red.

Round past the path's start

For the start (end) value, the text is rendered from the start (end) of the line until the initial position along the path is reached again.
