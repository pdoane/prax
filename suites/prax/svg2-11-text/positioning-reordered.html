SVG 2 · 11.2.2 Notes on 'x', 'y', 'dx', 'dy' and 'rotate'

Positions for reordered text

The following additional rules apply to attributes ‘x’, ‘y’, ‘dx’, ‘dy’, and ‘rotate’ when they contain a list of numbers. SVG 2 §11.2.2 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60, the baseline at y 45 dashed in red.

Reordered with the characters

Whenever the character data within a ‘tspan’ element is re-ordered, the corresponding elements within the ‘x’, ‘y’, ‘dx’, ‘dy’, and ‘rotate’ are also re-ordered to maintain the correspondence. Here "LLLT" in 30px PR Noto Sans Mono, 18 a letter, with direction: rtl; unicode-bidi: bidi-override, ending at x 150. Which edge of a right-to-left glyph it turns about is left open: its alignment point is on the start-edge of the glyph, its right, but the reordered text is positioned using standard left-to-right layout rules. Chrome turns it about its left edge, Firefox its right.
