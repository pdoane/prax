SVG 2 · 11.10.2.4 The ‘direction’ property

direction in SVG text

The property specifies the inline-base direction of a ‘text’ or ‘tspan’ element. It defines the "start" and "end" points of a line of text as used by the text-anchor and inline-size properties. It also may affect the direction in which characters are positioned if the unicode-bidi property's value is either embed or bidi-override.
