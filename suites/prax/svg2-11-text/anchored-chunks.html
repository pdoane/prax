Anchored chunks

The algorithm's "anchored chunk" flag, which represents whether it starts a contiguous sequence of glyphs that can be affected by text anchoring. A chunk's extent

The chunk's extent runs from its leftmost to its rightmost glyph edge, and the shift depends on the value of text-anchor and direction of the element the character at index i is in: (start, rtl) or (end, ltr): Set shift = shift − b.
