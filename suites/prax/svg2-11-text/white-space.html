SVG 2 · 11.10.3.1 SVG 2 Preferred white space handling, the ‘white-space’ property

white-space in SVG text

Rendering of white space in SVG 2 is controlled by the white-space property. This specifies two things: whether and how white space inside the element is collapsed; whether lines may wrap at unforced soft wrap opportunities.
