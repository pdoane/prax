Pointer Events · 7.3 Details of touch-action values

manipulation, and what touch-action leaves alone

The touch-action property covers direct manipulation behaviors related to viewport panning and zooming. Pointer Events §7.3

No double-tap zoom

The user agent MAY consider direct manipulation interactions that begin on the element only for the purposes of panning and continuous zooming (such as pinch-zoom), but MUST NOT trigger other related behaviors that rely on multiple activations that must happen within a set period of time (such as double-tap to zoom, or double-tap and hold for single-finger zoom). §7.3

Selection and activation are not its business

Any additional user agent behaviors, such as text selection/highlighting, or activating links and form controls, MUST NOT be affected by this CSS property. §7.3
