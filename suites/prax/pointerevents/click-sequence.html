Pointer Events · 11.3 Mouse Event Order

Clicks and double clicks

The following is the typical sequence of events when a button associated with a pointing device (e.g., a mouse button or trackpad) is pressed and released over an element: mousedown, mouseup, click, and again, then dblclick. Pointer Events §11.3

A double click

dblclick: This event type MUST be dispatched after the event type click if a click and double click occur simultaneously, and after the event type mouseup otherwise. §11.4.4

Pressed on one element, released on another

SHOULD fire click and dblclick events on the nearest common inclusive ancestor when the associated mousedown and mouseup event targets are different. §11.3
