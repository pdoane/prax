Pointer Events · 3.3.9 pointerleave

pointerleave

This event type is similar to pointerout but with two differences: pointerleave does not bubble, and its dispatch considers the hit test boundaries of even descendant elements. Pointer Events §3.3.9

Out of an element and all its descendants

The step to determine the target of a pointer detects that the pointer has moved out of an element and all of its descendants. §3.3.9

After a finger lifts

After the user agent fires the pointerup event for a device that does not support hover (see pointerup). §3.3.9
