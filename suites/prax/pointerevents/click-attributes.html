Pointer Events · 3.3.12.1 Event attributes

The attributes of a click

For these events, all PointerEvent specific attributes (defined in this spec) other than pointerId and pointerType MUST have their default values. Pointer Events §3.3.12.1

The pointer's identity, and no more

If the events are generated by a pointing device, their pointerId and pointerType MUST be the same as the PointerEvents that caused these events. §3.3.12.1

A click from the keyboard

If the events are generated by a non-pointing device (such as voice recognition software or a keyboard interaction), pointerId MUST be -1 and pointerType MUST be an empty string. §3.3.12.1
