CSS 2 · 12.2 The 'content' property

The content property: quote keywords

Every figure has quotes: "<" ">" "(" ")": angle brackets at the outer level, parentheses one level in. open-quote and close-quote are set on :before and :after of spans.

open-quote and close-quote

open-quote and close-quote: These values are replaced by the appropriate string from the 'quotes' property. CSS 2 §12.2

no-open-quote and no-close-quote

no-open-quote and no-close-quote: Introduces no content, but increments (decrements) the level of nesting for quotes. CSS 2 §12.2
