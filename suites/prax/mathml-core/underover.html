MathML Core · 3.4.2 Underscripts and Overscripts <munder>, <mover>, <munderover>

Underscripts and overscripts

The font-size is automatically scaled down within the scripts, unless they are meant to be accents. accent, accentunder attributes, if present, must have values that are booleans. If these attributes are absent or invalid, they are treated as equal to false. MathML Core §3.4.2
