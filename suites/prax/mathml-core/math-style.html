MathML Core · 4.3 The math-style property

math-style: normal and compact

When math-style is compact, the math layout on descendants tries to minimize the logical height: The font-size is scaled down when its specified value is math and the computed value of math-depth is auto-add, Operators with the largeop property do not follow rules […] to make them bigger., Under-/overscripts attached to an operator with the movablelimits property are actually drawn as sub-/superscripts, Smaller vertical gaps and shifts from the OpenType MATH table are used for fractions and radicals MathML Core §4.3
