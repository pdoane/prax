MathML Core · 3.3.6.2 Layout of <mpadded>

mpadded: lspace and voffset

The line-ascent of the math content is the requested height. The line-descent of the math content is the requested depth. The mpadded inner box is placed so that its alphabetic baseline is shifted away from the alphabetic baseline by the requested voffset towards the line-over. MathML Core §3.3.6.2 The box model figure places the inner box the requested lspace from the start. Each mpadded is 120px wide, 45px up and 35px down, around 1/(1 + x²) at 30px.
