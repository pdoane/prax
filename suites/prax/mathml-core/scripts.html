MathML Core · 3.4.1 Subscripts and Superscripts <msub>, <msup>, <msubsup>

Subscripts and superscripts

The msub, msup and msubsup elements are used to attach subscript and superscript to a MathML expression. The font-size is automatically scaled down within the scripts. MathML Core §3.4.1
