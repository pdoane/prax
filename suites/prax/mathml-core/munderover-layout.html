MathML Core · 3.4.2.5 Base with underscript and overscript

munderover: limits

For a base with the largeop property, UnderShift is the maximum of LowerLimitBaselineDropMin and LowerLimitGapMin + the ascent of the munder underscript, and OverShift the maximum of UpperLimitBaselineRiseMin and UpperLimitGapMin + the descent of the mover overscript. MathML Core §3.4.2.5 The formulas are set in PR Noto Sans Math, whose MATH constants shared/fonts.css lists in units of a 1000-unit em; a dashed blue line marks the height that the caption computes from them.
