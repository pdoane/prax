MathML Core · 3.3.1 Group Sub-Expressions <mrow>

mrow: a row on one baseline

The child boxes are put in a row one after the other with all their alphabetic baselines aligned. Because the box model ensures alignment of alphabetic baselines, fraction bars or symmetric stretchy operators will also be aligned along the math axis in the typical case when AxisHeight is the same for all in-flow children.
