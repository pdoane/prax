MathML Core · 3.3.2.1 Fraction with nonzero line thickness

mfrac: the bar on the math axis

The center of the fraction bar is shifted away from the alphabetic baseline of the math content box by a distance of AxisHeight towards the line-over. Its block size is the fraction line thickness. MathML Core §3.3.2.1 The formulas are set in PR Noto Sans Math, whose MATH constants shared/fonts.css lists in units of a 1000-unit em; a dashed blue line marks the height that the caption computes from them.
