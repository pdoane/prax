MathML Core · 3.4.1.1 Children of <msub>, <msup>, <msubsup>

Scripts with the wrong number of children

If the <msub> element has less or more than two in-flow children, its layout algorithm is the same as the mrow element. The same holds of msup, and of msubsup with other than three. MathML Core §3.4.1.1
