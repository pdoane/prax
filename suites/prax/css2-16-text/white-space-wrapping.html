CSS 2 · 16.6 White space: the 'white-space' property

The white-space property: values that wrap

white-space takes normal | pre | nowrap | pre-wrap | pre-line. Initial normal, inherited, applies to all elements. Each stage holds the same source: "one", three spaces, "two", a newline, then a sentence too long for one line. Stages clip what overflows.

pre-wrap and pre-line

This value prevents user agents from collapsing sequences of white space. Lines are broken at preserved newline characters, and as necessary to fill line boxes.
