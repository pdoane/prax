SVG 2 · 10.3 The ‘circle’ element

circle: center, radius and path

The ‘circle’ element defines a circle based on a center point and a radius. SVG 2 §10.3 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 80, in user units of 1px.

cx and cy, and r

The ‘cx’ and ‘cy’ attributes define the coordinates of the center of the circle. The ‘r’ attribute defines the radius of the circle. A computed value of zero disables rendering of the element.

Four arcs from 3 o'clock, clockwise

The path begins at the "3 o'clock" point on the radius and proceeds in a clock-wise direction (before any transformations). A light blue circle of radius 30 about 80, 40 is stroked with one orange dash, 6 wide, of a quarter of its outline, 47.12, followed by a gap longer than the outline.
