SVG 2 · 10.4 The ‘ellipse’ element

ellipse: its path, and where the stroke starts

Mathematically, an ‘ellipse’ element is mapped to an equivalent ‘path’ element that consists of four elliptical arc segments, each covering a quarter of the ellipse. SVG 2 §10.4 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 80.

Four arcs

A move-to command to the point cx+rx,cy; arc to cx,cy+ry; arc to cx-rx,cy; arc to cx,cy-ry; arc to cx+rx,cy; a closepath command.

From 3 o'clock, clockwise

The path begins at the "3 o'clock" point on the radius and proceeds in a clock-wise direction (before any transformation). A light blue ellipse, 60 by 30 about 80, 40, is stroked with one orange dash, 6 wide, of 40, followed by a gap longer than its outline.
