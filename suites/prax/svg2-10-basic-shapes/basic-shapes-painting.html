SVG 2 · 10.1 Introduction and definitions

Basic shapes: stroked, filled and clipping

The basic shapes may be stroked, filled and used as clip paths. All of the properties available for ‘path’ elements also apply to the basic shapes. SVG 2 §10.1 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60, in user units of 1px.
