SVG 2 · 10.2 The ‘rect’ element

rect: rounded corners

For rounded rectangles, the computed values of the ‘rx’ and ‘ry’ properties define the x- and y-axis radii of elliptical arcs used to round off the corners of the rectangle. SVG 2 §10.2 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60 holding a blue rectangle 120 by 40 at 20, 10.

Elliptical arcs, the same at every corner

The arc are always symmetrical along both horizontal and vertical axis; to create a rectangle with uneven corner rounding, define the shape explicitly with a ‘path’.

No rounding

A computed value of zero for either dimension, or a computed value of auto for both dimensions, results in a rectangle without corner rounding.
