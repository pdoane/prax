Procedure for rendering leaders

A leader is a row of these boxes, drawn from the end edge to the start edge, where only those boxes not overlaid by the before or after content. CSS Generated Content 3 §2.5.3 Each line is a span with ::after { content: leader(dotted) "9" }, in .pr-measure.

On the line where the before content ends

Lay out the before content, until reaching the line where the before content ends. On this line, draw the leader string, starting from the end edge, repeating as many times as possible until reaching the start edge. Draw the before and after content on top of the leader. If any part of the before content or after content overlaps a glyph in a leader string box, that glyph is not displayed.

No full copy: a line of its own

If one full copy of the leader string is not visible: Insert a line break after the before content, draw the leader on the next line, and draw the after content on top, and hide any leader strings that are not fully displayed. Also, A leader only appears on a single line, even if the before content and after content are on different lines. §2.5.2
