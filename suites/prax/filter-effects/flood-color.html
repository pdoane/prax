Filter Effects 1 · 9.13.1 The flood-color property

flood-color

The flood-color property indicates what color to used to flood the current filter primitive subregion. Filter Effects 1 §9.13.1 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 80 with a rectangle 100 by 40 at (30, 20); its filter is one feFlood, which fills the rectangle and 10% around it, from x 20 to 140 and y 16 to 64.

The color of the flood

A property, as well as an attribute

The flood-color property is a presentation attribute for SVG elements. A presentation attribute is an author declaration that any rule of a style sheet overrides.
