What forms a Backdrop Root

A Backdrop Root is formed, anywhere in the document, by an element in any of the following scenarios. Filter Effects 2 §3 This section, which says why for each, is non-normative, and the definition is open (fxtf-drafts#53). Each dashed box, 80 by 30, has backdrop-filter: grayscale(1), in a parent with no background over stripes: where the parent is a Backdrop Root, the stripes stay colored in the box. Safari's and Firefox's stills show no backdrop filter, as backdrop-filter.html says.
