Filter Effects 1 · 9.19 Filter primitive feSpecularLighting

feSpecularLighting

Sr = ks * pow(N.H, specularExponent) * Lr, and the same for green and blue; Sa = max(Sr, Sg, Sb). Filter Effects 1 §9.19 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60 with an opaque rectangle filling it, so the surface is flat and its normal N points at the viewer, (0, 0, 1); its filter region is the rectangle, in sRGB. The light is white.

Where the halfway vector meets the normal

H = (L + E) / Norm(L+E), with the eye E at (0, 0, 1). A point light at (80, 30), 40 above the surface, makes H the normal straight under it.

Not opaque

The alpha channel of the result is the max of the color components, so that where the specular light is zero, no additional coverage is added to the image and a fully white highlight will add opacity.
