Filter Effects 1 · 9.10 Filter primitive feDiffuseLighting

feDiffuseLighting: the alpha channel as a bump map

This filter primitive lights an image using the alpha channel as a bump map. The resulting image is an RGBA opaque image based on the light color with alpha = 1.0 everywhere. Filter Effects 1 §9.10 Each canvas, 160 by 40, lights an orange rectangle from x 40 to 120, y 10 to 30, with a distant light, in sRGB. Away from its edges the surface is flat, so Dr = kd * N.L * Lr with N straight up.

A flat surface: kd times N.L times the light

A slanting light, and surfaceScale

With azimuth 0 the light lies toward +x, Lx = cos(azimuth)*cos(elevation), and Z (x,y) = surfaceScale * I(x,y): the rectangle stands up from the canvas, its right side facing the light.
