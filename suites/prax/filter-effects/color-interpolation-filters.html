Filter Effects 1 · 10 The color-interpolation-filters property

color-interpolation-filters

The color-interpolation-filters property specifies the color space for imaging operations performed via filter effects. Filter Effects 1 §10 Each box is 60 by 30 and white before its filter, which halves each channel; the bar under it is the color expected.

linearRGB by default, or sRGB

linearRGB: Indicates that filter effects color operations should occur in the linear-light sRGB color space. Initial: linearRGB

Not filter functions

color-interpolation-filters has no affect for Filter Functions. Filter Functions must operate in the sRGB color space. §5
