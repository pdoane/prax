Filter Effects 1 · 9.13 Filter primitive feFlood

feFlood: a rectangle of one color

This filter primitive creates a rectangle filled with the color and opacity values from properties flood-color and flood-opacity. The rectangle is as large as the filter primitive subregion established by the feFlood element. Filter Effects 1 §9.13 Each stage is an SVG 200 by 80 with a blue rectangle 120 by 40 at (40, 20), outlined dashed; its filter floods it green.

As large as the subregion

With no x, y, width or height of its own, the subregion is the filter region, which by default reaches 10% of the rectangle past each side: 12px left and right, 4px above and below.

Its result is the rectangle

The flood replaces its input: what else shows is what the filter puts with it.
