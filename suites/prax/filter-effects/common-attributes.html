Filter Effects 1 · 9.2 Common filter primitive attributes

The common primitive attributes

The following filter primitive attributes are available for all filter primitives: x, y, width, height, result and in. Filter Effects 1 §9.2 Each canvas is 160 by 40, and is its filter's region.

x, y, width and height

result and in

In these, an orange flood is at x 0 to 40, a blue one at 60 to 100 or 120 to 160. Forward references to results are not allowed, and will be treated as if no result was specified.

The standard inputs

SourceAlpha: only the alpha channel is used, on black. FillPaint is the fill and has conceptually infinite extent.
