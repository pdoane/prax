Filter Effects 1 · 9.11 Filter primitive feDisplacementMap

feDisplacementMap: moving pixels by a map

P'(x,y) ← P( x + scale * (XC(x,y) - .5), y + scale * (YC(x,y) - .5)) Filter Effects 1 §9.11 Each canvas, 160 by 40, holds an orange rectangle from x 60 to 100, displaced by a map of one color.

The channel picks the distance

A pixel takes the color found scale × (XC − 0.5) to its right; where XC is 1 the image moves left by half the scale. The map's green and blue are 0, so with the scale at 40 a selector on either moves the image 20px.

Alpha by default, and the map's color space

Both selectors are A unless given. The color-interpolation-filters property only applies to the in2 source image: in the initial linearRGB, a red of 50% is 0.214.
