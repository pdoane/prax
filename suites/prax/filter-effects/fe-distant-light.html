Filter Effects 1 · 11.2 Light source feDistantLight

feDistantLight: azimuth and elevation

azimuth: Direction angle for the light source on the XY plane (clockwise), in degrees from the x axis. elevation: Direction angle for the light source from the XY plane towards the Z-axis, in degrees. Filter Effects 1 §11.2 Each filter is an feDiffuseLighting of a white light, in sRGB, so a flat surface facing the viewer takes the light's z component, Lz = sin(elevation), as its brightness.

Elevation, on a flat surface

Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60 filled by one opaque rectangle, the filter region its own.

Azimuth, on a raised disc

Each stage holds a disc of radius 20 at (80, 30), its alpha raised 10 by surfaceScale, lit from 45° up. A side facing the light is bright, the far side dark; the flat ground around the disc is grey. With no kernelUnitLength, the slope is taken over a pixel or so of the browser's own, so the rims' widths and depth differ between browsers; Safari's are fainter.
