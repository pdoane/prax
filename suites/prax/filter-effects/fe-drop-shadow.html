Filter Effects 1 · 9.12 Filter primitive feDropShadow

feDropShadow: a shadow of the input

This filter creates a drop shadow of the input image. It is a shorthand filter, and is defined in terms of combinations of other filter primitives. Filter Effects 1 §9.12 Each canvas, 160 by 40, holds an orange rectangle from x 20 to 80, y 5 to 25.

The shorthand and the tree it stands for

Blur the input's alpha, offset it by dx and dy, fill it with the flood color, then merge the result of the previous step under the input. A stdDeviation of 0 leaves the blur out.
