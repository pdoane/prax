Filter Effects 1 · 5 Graphic filters: the filter property

filter: all of the drawing, none of the geometry

Conceptually, any parts of the drawing are effected by filter operations. Filter Effects 1 §5

Everything the box draws, and nothing of its place

This includes any content, background, borders, text decoration, outline and visible scrolling mechanism of the element to which the filter is applied, and those of its descendants. The filter property has no effect on the geometry of the target element's CSS boxes.
