Filter Effects 1 · 11.3 Light source fePointLight

fePointLight

X location for the light source in the coordinate system established by attribute primitiveUnits on the filter element. Its brightness on a surface follows Lx = Lightx - x, Ly = Lighty - y, Lz = Lightz - Z(x,y). Filter Effects 1 §11.3 Each stage is an SVG 200 by 40 filled by one flat, opaque rectangle; its filter is an feDiffuseLighting of a white light, in sRGB, so a point's brightness is Lz over its distance from the light.
