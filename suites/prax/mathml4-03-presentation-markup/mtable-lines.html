MathML 4 · 3.5.1 Table or Matrix <mtable>

mtable: lines and frames

rowlines and columnlines: specifies whether and what kind of lines should be added between each row (column). frame: specifies whether and what kind of lines should be drawn around the table. framespacing: specifies the additional spacing added between the table and frame, if frame is not none.
