MathML 4 · 3.5.1 Table or Matrix <mtable>

mtable: columnalign and rowalign

rowalign: top aligns the tops of each entry across the row; bottom aligns the bottoms of the cells, center centers the cells; baseline aligns the baselines of the cells; axis aligns the axis of each cells. columnalign: left aligns the left side of the cells; center centers each cells; right aligns the right side of the cells.
