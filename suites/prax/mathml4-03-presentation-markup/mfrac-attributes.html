MathML 4 · 3.3.2 Fractions <mfrac>

mfrac: bevelled, numalign, denomalign

numalign: Specifies the alignment of the numerator over the fraction. bevelled: Specifies whether the fraction should be displayed in a bevelled style (the numerator slightly raised, the denominator slightly lowered and both separated by a slash), rather than "build up" vertically. The default value is medium; thin is thinner, but visible; thick is thicker. MathML 4 §3.3.2
