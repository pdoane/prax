MathML 4 · 3.3.9 Enclose Expression Inside Notation <menclose>

menclose: enclosures

The menclose element renders its content inside the enclosing notation specified by its notation attribute. The values left, right, top and bottom should result in lines drawn on those sides of the contents. MathML 4 §3.3.9 Each encloses "x + y".
