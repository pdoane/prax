SVG 2 · 12.2 Placement of the embedded content

The positioning rectangle

The 'x', 'y', width, and height geometry properties specify the rectangular region into which the embedded content is positioned (the positioning rectangle). SVG 2 §12.2 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60; the images are the flag 40 by 20 in quarters, blue, orange, green, purple. A dashed grey outline marks each positioning rectangle.

From attributes or from CSS

Each image has preserveAspectRatio="none", so the flag fills the rectangle.

The bounding box

The positioning rectangle is used as the bounding box of the element, whatever part of it the image covers. Each image is 120 by 40 at 20, 10, with preserveAspectRatio="xMinYMid meet", so the flag is 80 by 40 at its left, and is clipped by a clipPath in objectBoundingBox units.
