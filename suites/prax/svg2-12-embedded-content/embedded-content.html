SVG 2 · 12.1 Overview

Embedded content: image and foreignObject

Embedded content is content that imports another resource into the document, or content from another vocabulary that is inserted into the document. SVG 2 §12.1 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60. The images are a flag 40 by 20 in quarters: blue and orange above, green and purple below.

Another resource, imported with image

SVG supports embedded content with the use of ‘image’ and ‘foreignObject’ elements. Each image is at 40, 10, 80 by 40.

Another vocabulary, inserted with foreignObject

A foreignObject at 20, 10, 120 by 40, holds HTML: two block boxes, A and B, each a line 20 tall.
