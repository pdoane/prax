SVG 2 · 12.3 The ‘image’ element

image: the SVG's viewport

When the referenced image is an SVG, the image-rendering rectangle defines the SVG viewport used for rendering that SVG. SVG 2 §12.3 The image is an SVG with no size of its own, whose rect is 50vw by 50vh: half of its viewport on each axis.

Viewport-relative lengths are of the rectangle the image is drawn in

The image is drawn 120 by 40, outlined dashed grey, so its viewport is 120 by 40 and its rect 60 by 20, wherever it is shown.
