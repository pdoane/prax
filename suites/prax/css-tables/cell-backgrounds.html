CSS Table 3 · 5.3.2 Drawing cell backgrounds

Cell backgrounds: six layers

In addition to its own 'background', table-cell boxes also render the backgrounds of the table-track and table-track-group boxes in which they belong. the 'background-origin' and 'background-size' computations will actually be done on the bounds of the grouping boxes, and not on those of the cell. Bottom to top

The background set in one of the layers will only be visible if the layers above it have a transparent background. From the bottom: column group, column, row group, row, cell; the table's own is below them all.
