CSS Table 3 · 5.4.2 Rendering a visibility: collapse table-track or table-track-group

visibility: collapse on a track: backgrounds stay

When a table-track or table-track-group has visibility: collapse, all the backgrounds, borders or outlines that are contributed by the cells within the given table-track or table-track-group will continue to be painted on cells that have not been fully collapsed (because they spanned multiple tracks).
