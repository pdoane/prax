CSS Table 3 · 5.3.2 Drawing cell backgrounds, areas

Cell backgrounds: originating tracks, and their areas

A cell draws the backgrounds of its originating column group, column, row group and row. For positioning, it is expected that a row occupies the largest possible area a single cell could occupy in the row/column grid while originating in the row and not entering any other row.
