CSS Table 3 · 3.9.3 Distribution algorithm

Width distribution: sizing types and guesses

Each column is a percent-column, a pixel-column or an auto-column. Four sizing-guesses follow: min-content (every column its min-content width); min-content-percentage (percent-columns the larger of: its intrinsic percentage width times the assignable width and its min-content width); min-content-specified (also any other column that is constrained is assigned its max-content width); and max-content. If the assignable table width is less than or equal to the max-content sizing-guess, the used widths of the columns must be the linear combination (with weights adding to 1) of the two consecutive sizing-guesses whose width sums bound the available width. Three columns, four guesses

Green "a" is width: 25%, a percent-column; orange "b" width: 50px, a pixel-column; blue "aa bbbb" an auto-column, 40px at least and 70px on one line (.pr-measure). At a table width W, the guesses are 10 + 10 + 40 = 60px; W/4 + 10 + 40; W/4 + 50 + 40; and W/4 + 50 + 70.
