CSS Table 3 · 3.8.2 Computing Cell Measures, offsets

Cell offsets, box-sizing and collapsed borders

The cell intrinsic offsets are the cell's horizontal padding and border: In separated-borders mode: the computed horizontal padding and border of the table-cell; In collapsed-borders mode: the computed horizontal padding of the cell and, for border values, the used border-width values of the cell (half the winning border-width). box-sizing on a cell, not on a column

For cells, the widths are their computed values from which the cell's border-{left|right}-width and/or padding-{left|right} have eventually been deduced, depending on the value of 'box-sizing'; for columns, their computed value, irrespective of the value of 'box-sizing' applied on the element.
