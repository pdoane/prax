Where ink skipping breaks a line

When the UA interrupts underlines or overlines at glyph boundaries, the shape of the line at that boundary should follow the shape of the glyph. CSS Text Decoration 4 §2.10.5 A note leaves the method to the browser: square ends, trapezoids, or the curve itself.
