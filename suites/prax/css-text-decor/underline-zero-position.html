CSS Text Decoration 4 · 2.8.1 Underline Offset Origin (Zero Position)

Where the offset starts

The zero position of the underline depends on the value of 'text-underline-position'. The underline is aligned to the outside of the specified position (extending its thickness in the positive direction only). CSS Text Decoration 4 §2.8.1 Every underline here has text-underline-offset: 0 and is 6px thick, translucent red; red dashes mark the baseline.

Horizontal: the baseline, the font's position, the text-under edge

Vertical: the left edge grows left, the right edge grows right

left: text-under (left) edge, positive under; right: text-over (right) edge, positive over. Their edges are the edges of the em box.
