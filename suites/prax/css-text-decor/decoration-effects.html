The shadow of a decoration is the descendant's shadow

Unlike line decorations, however, `text-shadow` is inherited as a property; therefore the green text shadow on the <span> overrides the blue text shadow on the <div>. As a result, when the shadows are painted, the shadow of the <div>’s underline is disjoint across the two elements. The example, halved: the div is 24px, underlined, with a blue shadow 25px down; the span is 10px, vertical-align: top, with a green shadow 50px down.
