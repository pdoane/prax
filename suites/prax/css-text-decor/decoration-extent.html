CSS Text Decoration 4 · 2 Line Decoration: Underline, Overline, and Strike-Through

What a line decoration crosses

Underlines, overlines, and line-throughs are drawn only for non-replaced inline boxes, and are drawn across all text (including white space, letter spacing, and word spacing) except spacing (white space, letter spacing, and word spacing) at the beginning and end of a line. Across spacing, not at the ends of a line

The decorating box's margins, borders and padding are skipped; a descendant's are not

Margins, borders, and padding of the decorating box are always skipped, however the margins, border, and padding of descendant inline boxes are not.
