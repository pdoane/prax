Color stop lists: positions

Lengths are measured along the gradient line from the starting point in the direction of the ending point. CSS Images 4 §3.5.1 Each bar is to right and 200px wide, so 10% is one guide.

Lengths and percentages

Two positions, two stops

A color stop with two positions is equivalent to specifying two color stops with the same color, one for each position.

Positions left out

it's assigned the position halfway between the two surrounding stops. If multiple stops in a row lack a position, they space themselves out equally between the surrounding positioned stops.
