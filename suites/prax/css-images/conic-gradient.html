conic-gradient(): colors around a center

Rays are then drawn emerging from the center and pointing in all directions, with the color of each ray equal to the color of the gradient-line where they intersect it. CSS Images 4 §3.3 Each box is 180 by 80, its center at 100, 50 on the guides.

Each ray one color

Stops two to a position change color at once, so each quarter of the turn is one color.
