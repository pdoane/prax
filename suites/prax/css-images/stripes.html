stripes(): how thick each stripe is

Stripes are placed end-to-end on the paint line in the order given. CSS Images 4 §4 Each box has a top border 40px wide whose border-color is the stripes, the first at the bottom, by the light orange padding box.

Lengths, percentages and fr

A <flex> is evaluated as a fraction of the total width relative to the total sum of <flex> entries in the function, after subtracting the thickness of any non-<flex> entries; If the sum of <flex> values is less than 1fr, the result of the subtraction is multiplied by the sum's value.

Too little, and too much

If the sum of the stripes is smaller than the total width, the paint line is transparent black for its remaining length. If the sum is larger, any stripes or portions beyond the total width are truncated.
