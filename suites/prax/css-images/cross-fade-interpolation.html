Interpolating cross-fade()

The three components of cross-fade() are interpolated independently. Note this may result in nested cross-fade() notations. CSS Images 4 §7.3 The text dates from a cross-fade() of two images and a percentage; with the same images on both sides, the percentages interpolate.
