SVG 2 · 13.7.1 The ‘marker’ element

marker: viewBox, refX and refY

The ‘markerWidth’ and ‘markerHeight’ attributes represent the size of the SVG viewport into which the marker is to be fitted according to the ‘viewBox’ and ‘preserveAspectRatio’ attributes. SVG 2 §13.7.1 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60 with a line from 20, 30 to 140, 30 whose middle vertex, at 80, 30, has a marker-mid 20 by 20 in user units. The marker's content is an orange rectangle the size of its viewBox.

Fitted to the viewport

The reference point

The ‘refX’ and ‘refY’ attributes define the reference point of the marker, which is to be placed exactly at the marker's position on the shape. Percentage values must be interpreted as being a percentage of the ‘viewBox’ width for ‘refX’ or a percentage of the ‘viewBox’ height for ‘refY’. The keywords are left 0%, center 50%, right 100%, top 0% and bottom 100%. Each viewBox is 10 by 10. The working group resolved on both in svgwg#64.
