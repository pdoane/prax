SVG 2 · 13.7.3 Marker shorthand: the ‘marker’ property

The marker shorthand

The 'marker' property sets values for the 'marker-start', 'marker-mid' and 'marker-end' properties. The value of the 'marker' is used directly for all three of the corresponding longhand properties.
