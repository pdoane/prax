SVG 2 · 13.5.1 Specifying stroke paint: the ‘stroke’ property

stroke: along the outline

The 'stroke' property paints along the outline of the given graphical element. SVG 2 §13.5.1 Its value is a paint §13.2, and its initial value none. Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60.

A band on the outline

A stroke 10 wide reaches 5 to each side of the outline §13.5.7. The rectangle is 120 by 40 at 20, 10, filled blue.

Open subpaths, and text

Unlike the fill, the stroke follows only the outline as drawn.
