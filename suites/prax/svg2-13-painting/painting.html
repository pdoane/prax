SVG 2 · 13.1 Introduction

Painting: filled, stroked and marked

Graphical elements that define a shape – ‘path’ elements, basic shapes, and text content elements – are rendered by being filled, which is painting the interior of the object, and stroked, which is painting along the outline of the object. SVG 2 §13.1 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60, in user units of 1px.

Filled and stroked

Filling and stroking are both painting operations.

Marker symbols

Some graphics elements – ‘path’ elements and basic shapes – can also have marker symbols drawn at their vertices or at other positions along the path that they describe. The marker is an orange dot 12 across at half opacity, so that two on one vertex are darker than one.
