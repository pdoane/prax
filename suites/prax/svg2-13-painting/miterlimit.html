SVG 2 · 13.5.5 Controlling line joins: the ‘stroke-linejoin’ and ‘stroke-miterlimit’ properties

stroke-miterlimit

The 'stroke-miterlimit' imposes a limit on the extent of the line join. given the angle θ between the segments in user coordinate system, the miter length is calculated by: miter length = stroke-width / sin(theta / 2). SVG 2 §13.5.5 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60; the joins are miter, and a white line 1 wide over each stroke is the path itself.

Past the limit, a bevel

If the miter length divided by the stroke width exceeds the 'stroke-miterlimit' then for the value: miter the join is converted to a bevel. The initial limit is 4.

A limit of 1, and curves

The join is built on the tangents: Let A be the line parallel to the tangent at the end of the segment. Let B be the line parallel to the tangent at the start of the following segment. §13.5.7
