SVG 2 · 13.3 The effect of the ‘color’ property

The color property and currentColor

The color property is used to provide a potential indirect value, currentColor, for the 'fill', 'stroke', stop-color, flood-color and lighting-color properties. The property has no other effect on SVG elements. SVG 2 §13.3 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60.

currentColor

No other effect

The initial value of fill is black §13.4.1, and color does not change it.
