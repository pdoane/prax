SVG 2 · 13.5.4 Drawing caps at the ends of strokes: the ‘stroke-linecap’ property

stroke-linecap

'stroke-linecap' specifies the shape to be used at the end of open subpaths when they are stroked. SVG 2 §13.5.4 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60; a white line 1 wide over each stroke is the path itself. Caps on zero-length subpaths are on 9.5.3's page.

butt, round and square

The path runs from x 40 to 120 along y 30, stroked blue 20 wide.

At the ends of open subpaths only

at each end of each subpath, in the direction of the path there. The strokes are 10 wide.
