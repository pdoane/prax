SVG 2 · 13.4.3 Fill paint opacity: the ‘fill-opacity’ property

fill-opacity

'fill-opacity' specifies the opacity of the painting operation used to paint the fill the current object. SVG 2 §13.4.3 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60; all but the last have an orange band 10 tall across them at y 25, under a blue rectangle 120 by 40 at 20, 10.

The opacity of the fill alone

<number>: The opacity of the fill. <percentage>: The opacity of the fill expressed as a percentage of the range 0 to 1.

Clamped, and inherited

Any values outside the range 0 (fully transparent) to 1 (fully opaque) must be clamped to this range. The property is inherited, and each element's fill is painted at its opacity.
