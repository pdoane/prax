SVG 2 · 13.4.1 Specifying fill paint: the ‘fill’ property

fill: the interior

The 'fill' property paints the interior of the given graphical element. The area to be painted consists of any areas inside the outline of the shape. SVG 2 §13.4.1 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60.

Open subpaths, filled as if closed

The fill operation fills open subpaths by performing the fill operation as if an additional "closepath" command were added to the path to connect the last point of the subpath with the first point of the subpath. The shapes are filled blue and stroked dark blue, 2 wide.

Shapes and text

The initial value is black, and fill applies to shapes and text content elements.
