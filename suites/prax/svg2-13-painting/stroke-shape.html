SVG 2 · 13.5.7 Computing the shape of the stroke

The stroke shape: dashes, caps and joins

The stroke shape of an element is the shape that is filled by the 'stroke' property. It is built dash by dash, each from its dash positions. SVG 2 §13.5.7 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60; the strokes are blue, and a white line 1 wide over each is the path itself.

Caps on every dash

Set dash to be the union of dash and the starting cap shape for the subpath at position start. And the ending cap at end, even where a dash has no length.

Joins inside a dash, and a dash past the end

While index < last: Set dash to be the union of dash and the line join shape for the subpath at segment index index. Let dashlength be min(sum(dashesi, 0 ≤ i ≤ index) − offset, pathlength).
