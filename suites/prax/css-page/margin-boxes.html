CSS Paged Media 3 · 5 Page-Margin Boxes

The sixteen page-margin boxes

The margin area of a page box is divided into 16 page-margin boxes. §3 Page-margin boxes are positioned with respect to the page area and are independent of page orientation CSS Paged Media 3 §5 Safari paints the root's background on the last page only as far down as the content.

Corners, sides, and three boxes on each side

Each box here has content: its initials, white on a color. A corner box is a fixed-size box defined by the intersection of the two margins; top-center is a variable-width box centered horizontally between the page's left and right border edges.
