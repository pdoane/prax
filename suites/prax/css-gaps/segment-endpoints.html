CSS Gaps 1 · 3.1.1 Segment endpoints

Segment endpoints

A segment endpoint is a point on a gap's centerline where a gap decoration segment may start or end. CSS Gaps 1 §3.1.1

At the bounds of a gap, and on each side of a junction

Endpoints are made at a gap's two bounds, and at each gap junction, a region of G where one or more gaps abut or overlap G: an end endpoint where a cross gap appears on at least one side of G, a start endpoint where no cross gap exists on either side of G. With rule-break: intersection, a rule runs from each start endpoint to the next end endpoint. Column rules are red, row rules black.

Flex lines and rows of columns

Each flex line has column gaps of its own, which abut the row gap between the lines.
