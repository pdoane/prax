Gaps, alignment and breaks

Gutters effect a minimum spacing between items: additional spacing may be added by 'justify-content'/'align-content'. Such additional space increases the size of the corresponding gaps. CSS Gaps 1 §2

Distributed space widens the gap

Each stage has three 40px items and column-gap: 10px; where gap decorations are drawn, a red column-rule marks the middle of each gap.

A gap at a break disappears

In all cases, the gap disappears when it coincides with a fragmentation break. The stage is a multicol container 50px tall, filled column by column, holding a grid of 20px rows with row-gap: 10px.
