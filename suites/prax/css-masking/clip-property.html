The deprecated clip property

With this specification the clip property is deprecated. Authors are encouraged to use the clip-path property instead. UAs must support the clip property. CSS Masking 1 §A Each blue box is absolutely positioned at 10, 10 in its stage unless said otherwise.

Offsets from the top and left border edges

top and bottom specify offsets from the top border edge of the box, and right, and left specify offsets from the left border edge of the box.

Absolutely positioned boxes only, and negative lengths
