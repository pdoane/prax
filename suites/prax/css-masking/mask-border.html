CSS Masking 1 · 8 Border-Box Mask

Border-box mask: nine pieces as a mask

With mask-border an image can be split into nine pieces: four corners, four edges and the middle piece. These pieces may be sliced, scaled and stretched in various ways to fit the size of the mask border image area. This distorted image is then used as a mask. CSS Masking 1 §8

The image, and the box it masks

The image is 30 by 30: opaque discs in its 10px corners, 4px bars along its edges and a 4px square in the middle, on transparency. Each box is 140 by 60 and blue.
