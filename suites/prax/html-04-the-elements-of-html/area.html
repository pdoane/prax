HTML · 4.8.13 The area element

The area element

The area element represents either a hyperlink with some text and a corresponding area on an image map, or a dead area on an image map. the shape and coords attributes specify the area. HTML §4.8.13 Each image is 200 by 100, a blue rectangle from 10, 10 to 60, 90, an orange circle at 100, 50 of radius 30, and a green triangle, and each area matches one of them. Each log lists the target of the clicks in its stage.

A rectangle, a circle and a polygon

Rectangle: the distance from the left edge of the image to the left side of the rectangle, the distance from the top edge to the top side, the distance from the left edge to the right side, and the distance from the top edge to the bottom side. Circle: the center's distance in CSS pixels from the left edge and from the top edge, then the radius of the circle. Polygon: the points of the polygon, in order.
