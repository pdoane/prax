HTML · 4.10.18.3 Association of controls and forms

The form owner

A form-associated element is, by default, associated with its nearest ancestor form element, but, if it is listed, may have a form attribute specified to override this. HTML §4.10.18.3

The form attribute

If the first element in element's tree, in tree order, to have an ID that is identical to element's form content attribute's value, is a form element, then associate the element with that form element. The dashed box is the form first; the form second is empty and hidden. §4.10.18.3
