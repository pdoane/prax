HTML · 4.8.8 The video element

The video element

A video element represents what is given for the first matching condition in the list below HTML §4.8.8 The video, clip.mp4, is 160 by 80 and two seconds long: blue, orange, green, purple, half a second each. The poster is blue, 80 by 40. Each video is outlined in dashed red.

Before any video data: the poster, or nothing

When no video data is available, The video element represents its poster frame, if any, or else transparent black with no natural dimensions. The playback area's natural size is the natural width of the poster frame, if that is available and the element currently represents its poster frame.

Paused at the start: the poster, or the first frame

When the video element is paused, the current playback position is the first frame of video, and the element's show poster flag is set, The video element represents its poster frame, if any, or else the first frame of the video.
