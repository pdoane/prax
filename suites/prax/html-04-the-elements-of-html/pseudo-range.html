HTML · 4.16.3 Pseudo-classes

:in-range and :out-of-range

:in-range matches all elements that are candidates for constraint validation, have range limitations, and that are neither suffering from an underflow nor suffering from an overflow, and :out-of-range those with range limitations that are suffering from either. HTML §4.16.3

Within min and max, or not

Borders: green :in-range, orange :out-of-range, grey neither. §4.16.3
