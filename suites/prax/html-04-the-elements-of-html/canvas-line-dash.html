HTML · 4.12.5.1.4 Line styles

Dashed lines

setLineDash Sets the current line dash pattern (as used when stroking). The argument is a list of distances for which to alternately have the line on and the line off. HTML §4.12.5.1.4 Every line here runs from x 10 to 190, 6 wide, butt-capped unless a caption says otherwise.

On, off, odd lists, and the phase

If the number of elements in segments is odd, then let segments be the concatenation of two copies of segments. Let position be 0 minus offset.

Caps on every dash, and dashes along the whole subpath

Points with no lines coming out of them must have two caps placed back-to-back.
