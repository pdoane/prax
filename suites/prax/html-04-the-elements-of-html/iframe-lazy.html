HTML · 4.8.5 The iframe element

Lazy loading an iframe

The loading attribute is a lazy loading attribute. Its purpose is to indicate the policy for loading iframe elements that are outside the viewport. HTML §4.8.5 It is navigated once it comes within the implementation-defined lazy load scroll margin of the viewport. Each iframe is 6000px down a scroller, and the page's script gives it a green srcdoc; the log lists its load.

Navigated when scrolled to, or when made eager
