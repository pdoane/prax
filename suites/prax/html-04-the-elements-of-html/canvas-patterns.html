Canvas patterns

createPattern Returns a CanvasPattern object that uses the given image and repeats in the direction(s) given. HTML §4.12.5.1.10 The image is the 40 by 20 flag. Each pattern fills a rectangle from (10, 10) to (190, 50), outlined in red.

Repetition, from the origin, and the pattern's matrix

Place a copy of the image on the bitmap, anchored such that its top left corner is at the origin of the coordinate space, then repeat it as the repetition says. Transform the resulting bitmap according to the pattern's transformation matrix.
