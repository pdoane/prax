SVG 2 · 3.2.2 Rendered versus non-rendered elements

Rendered and non-rendered elements

Whether an element is currently rendered or not affects not only its visual display but also interactivity and geometric calculations. SVG 2 §3.2.2 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60; each non-rendered element here has display="none".

No effect on clip paths, bounding boxes or text

Non-rendered elements do not contribute to the net geometry of clipping paths or masks when they are descendants of a ‘clipPath’ or ‘mask’, do not contribute to bounding box calculations, and are not considered when performing text layout.

Not sensitive to pointer events

They are not sensitive to pointer events. A blue rect, and over it a non-rendered one with a listener logged below.
