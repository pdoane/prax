SVG 2 · 3.7.1 Painting shapes and text

Painting shapes and text

The order in which fill, stroke and markers are painted is determined by the ‘paint-order’ property. The default is that fill is painted first, then the stroke, and then the marker symbols. The marker symbols are rendered in order along the outline of the shape, from the start of the shape to the end of the shape. SVG 2 §3.7.1 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60.

Independent fill and stroke

The fill and stroke operations are entirely independent; for instance, each fill or stroke operation has its own opacity setting.
