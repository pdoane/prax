CSS Backgrounds 4 · 2.5 Affixing Images: the background-attachment property

background-attachment: local

local: The background is fixed with regard to the box's contents: if the box is a scroll container, the background scrolls with the box's contents, and the background painting area and background positioning area are relative to the scrollable overflow area of the box rather than to the border framing them.
