CSS Backgrounds 4 · 2.8 Painting Area: the background-clip property

background-clip: text and border-area

text: The background is painted within (clipped to) the intersection of the border box and the geometry of the text including any decorations and emphasis marks in the element and its in-flow and floated descendants. The text color has no effect on the painting of the background. CSS Backgrounds 4 §2.8

A gradient from blue to orange, and transparent text.

border-area

border-area: The background is clipped to the area painted by the border, taking 'border-width' and 'border-style' into account but ignoring any transparency introduced by 'border-color'. The box has a faint 10px dashed border.
