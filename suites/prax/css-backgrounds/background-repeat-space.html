CSS Backgrounds 4 · 2.3 Tiling Images, space

background-repeat: space

The image is repeated in the given direction as often as will fit within the background positioning area without being clipped and then the repeated images are spaced out to fill the area. The first and last images touch the edges of the area. CSS Backgrounds 4 §2.3 Dashed: the positioning area.

Whole images, spaced out, or one placed

Where there is not enough space for two copies of the image in this direction, in which case only one image is placed and 'background-position' determines its position in this direction.

The pattern carries on into the painting area

If the background painting area is larger than the background positioning area, then the pattern repeats to fill the background painting area.
