Subgrids: their margins, borders and padding

the sum of the subgrid's margin, padding, scrollbar gutter, and border at each edge are applied as an extra layer of (potentially negative) margin to the items at those edges. This extra layer of “margin” accumulates through multiple levels of subgrids. CSS Grid Layout 2 §9
