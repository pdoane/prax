CSS Grid Layout 2 · 11.2 Aligning with auto margins

auto margins on grid items

Auto margins on grid items have an effect very similar to auto margins in block layout. CSS Grid Layout 2 §11.2

Zero for sizing, then absorbing free space

During calculations of grid track sizes, auto margins are treated as 0. Then auto margins in either axis absorb positive free space prior to alignment via the box alignment properties, thereby disabling the effects of any self-alignment properties in that axis.

Overflowing items

Overflowing grid items resolve their auto margins to zero and overflow as specified by their box alignment properties. Blue is 140px wide in the 100px column at 60px, with margin-left: auto.
