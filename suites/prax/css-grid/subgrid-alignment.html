CSS Grid Layout 2 · 9 Subgrids, alignment, overflow and positioning

Subgrids: stretched, aligned, clipped and shifted

The subgrid is always stretched in its subgridded dimension(s): the align-self/justify-self properties on it are ignored, as are any specified width/height constraints. CSS Grid Layout 2 §9 Each parent grid has columns of 40, 60 and 80px; the tinted, outlined box is a subgrid of its columns 2 to 4, and A is in its first column.

Its own size and alignment are ignored

Layoutwise, the subgrid’s grid is always aligned with the corresponding section of the parent grid; the align-content/justify-content properties on it are also ignored in the subgridded dimension.

Overflow and relative positioning

The overflow property does apply to subgrids, so that overflowing contents of the subgrid can be scrolled into view. Relative positioning applies to subgrids as normal, and shifts the box and its content together as usual. C, under the subgrid, is in the parent's second column.
