Subgrids: padding at an empty edge

For each edge of a non-empty subgrid, to account for the subgrid’s margin/border/padding (and any scrollbar gutter) at that edge, a hypothetical item is contributed to the track sizing algorithm for each span size in the set of items spanning into the occupied track closest to that edge of the subgrid. CSS Grid Layout 2 §9

The item is not in the edge track

The hypothetical item’s sizes are taken from the sizes of the largest such item of each span size, and are additionally inflated by the subgrid’s own margin/border/padding/gutter at that edge. Correspondingly, the hypothetical item’s span is taken from that same real item’s span, and inflated by the number of empty tracks between it and the relevant subgrid’s edge(s). The parent has three auto columns; the tinted, outlined subgrid spans them, and holds only "abcd", 40px, in its middle column.

Rows

The parent has two 100px columns and three auto rows. In the first column, a subgrid of the three rows holds "aa" in its middle row; in the second, empty orange and green boxes stretch to the first and last rows.
