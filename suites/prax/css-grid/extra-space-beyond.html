Into base sizes

For minimum and min-content contributions, any affected track that happens to also have an intrinsic max track sizing function; if there are no such tracks, then all affected tracks. Blue is "abcdef", 60px; each grid is 10px wide (dashed), so its columns are their base sizes.

Into growth limits, and fit-content()

For growth limits, any affected track that has an intrinsic max track sizing function. A fit-content() track is treated as max-content until the track reaches the limit specified as the fit-content() argument. Blue is "abc def gh", 30px min-content, 100px max-content; in 150px, the columns grow to their growth limits §12.6.
