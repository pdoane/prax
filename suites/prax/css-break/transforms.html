CSS Fragmentation 4 · 5.5 Transforms, Positioning, and Pagination

Transforms and positioning per fragment

Fragmentation interacts with layout, and thus occurs before relative positioning, transforms, and any other graphical effects. Such effects are applied per fragment: for example, rotation applied to a fragmented box will calculate a rotation origin for each fragment and independently rotate that fragment around its origin.
