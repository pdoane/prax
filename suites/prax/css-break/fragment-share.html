CSS Fragmentation 4 · 2 Fragmentation Model and Terminology

Each fragment has its share of the box

A fragmentainer is A box—such as a page box, column box, or region—that contains a portion (or all) of a fragmented flow. When breakable content would overflow a fragmentainer in the block dimension, it breaks into the next container in its fragmentation context instead. CSS Fragmentation 4 §2

Each fragment has its share of the box

Each fragment has its own share of the box’s border, padding, and margin, and therefore has its own padding area, border area, and margin area. The box has a 10px margin, a 4px border and 6px of padding, top and bottom.
