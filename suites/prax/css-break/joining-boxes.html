Joining fragments for slice

For box-decoration-break: slice, backgrounds (and border-image) are drawn as if applied to a composite box consisting of all of the box’s fragments reassembled in visual order. CSS Fragmentation 4 §5.4.1 Each background is a gradient from white to blue.

Across lines

fragments on subsequent lines are ordered according to the element’s inline base direction. The span runs over two lines, 200px wide; the gradient runs from left to right over the composite box.

Across columns and pages

Fragments are connected as if the column boxes were glued together in the block flow direction of the multi-column container. The gradient runs from top to bottom.
