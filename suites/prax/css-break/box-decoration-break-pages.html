CSS Fragmentation 4 · 5.4 Fragmented Borders and Backgrounds: the box-decoration-break property

box-decoration-break on pages

When a break (page/column/region/line) splits a box, the box-decoration-break property controls how its decorations wrap the broken edges. CSS Fragmentation 4 §5.4

The box has a 4px border, 6px of padding and 12px radii, and holds nine lines.

Breaks a block imposes on an inline

UAs should also apply box-decoration-break to control rendering at bidi-imposed breaks and/or at display-type–imposed breaks. Otherwise such breaks must be handled as slice. The span has a 2px border and 10px of padding at each side, and holds a block, B, between two words. Bidi breaks are shown in box model of reordering-induced box fragments.
