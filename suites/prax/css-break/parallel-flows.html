CSS Fragmentation 4 · 2.1 Parallel Fragmentation Flows

Parallel flows break on their own

When multiple formatting contexts are laid out parallel to each other, fragmentation is performed independently in each formatting context. UAs may (but are not required to) adjust the placement of unforced breaks in parallel formatting contexts to visually balance such side-by-side content, but must not do so to match a forced break.
