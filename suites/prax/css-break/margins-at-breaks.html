CSS Fragmentation 4 · 5.2 Adjoining Margins at Breaks: the margin-break property

Margins at column breaks

margin-break: auto, the initial value: When an unforced break occurs before or after the box, any margins adjoining the break truncate to the remaining fragmentainer extent before the break, and are truncated to zero after the break. When a forced break occurs there, adjoining margins before the break are truncated, but margins after the break are preserved. CSS Fragmentation 4 §5.2
