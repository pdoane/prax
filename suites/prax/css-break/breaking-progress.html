CSS Fragmentation 4 · 4 Rules for Breaking

Fragmentainers at least 1px tall

A fragmented flow may be broken across fragmentainers at a number of possible break points. In the case of forced breaks, the UA is required to break the flow at that point. In the case of unforced breaks, the UA has to choose among the possible breaks that are allowed. CSS Fragmentation 4 §4

Progress

To guarantee progress, fragmentainers are assumed to have a minimum block size of 1px regardless of their used size. A multi-column container 0px tall (height: 0; column-fill: auto), three columns, holds three lines.
