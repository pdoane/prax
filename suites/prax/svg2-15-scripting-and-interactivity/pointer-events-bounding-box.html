Clipped, masked, filtered

pointer events must be captured normally over the rendered areas of a clipped element, but must not be captured over the clipped areas; for elements with a mask applied, pointer events must still be captured even in areas where the mask goes to zero opacity. The filter property has no effect on pointer events processing.
