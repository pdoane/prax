CSS Color HDR 1 · 3.1 The dynamic-range-limit property

dynamic-range-limit, inherited

The property is Inherited: yes, and limits colors as well as images. CSS Color HDR 1 §3.1

Inherited, and on a color

The image is four gray steps encoded in PQ, at 100, 500, 1,000 and 5,000 cd/m², under a white bar.
