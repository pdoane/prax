SVG 2 · 7.2 Vertical center coordinate: The ‘cy’ property

cy: the vertical center

The ‘cy’ property describes the vertical center coordinate of the position of the element. SVG 2 §7.2 It applies to circle and ellipse. Each stage is an SVG 160 by 80, in user units of 1px.

A property, from CSS, and animated

A style sheet's cy: 60px over cy="20". Its animation type is by computed value.

A percentage, of the viewport's height, or negative

Percentages refer to the height of the current SVG viewport. Negative values are permitted for ‘cy’; a negative value translates the center of the shape and does not disable rendering.
