SVG 2 · 7.1 Horizontal center coordinate: The ‘cx’ property

cx: the horizontal center

The ‘cx’ property describes the horizontal center coordinate of the position of the element. SVG 2 §7.1 It applies to circle and ellipse. Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60, in user units of 1px.

A property, from CSS, and animated

A style sheet's cx: 110px over cx="40". Its animation type is by computed value.

A percentage, of the viewport's width, or negative

Percentages refer to the width of the current SVG viewport. Negative values are permitted for ‘cx’; a negative value translates the center of the shape and does not disable rendering.
