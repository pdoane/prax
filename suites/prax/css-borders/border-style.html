CSS Borders and Box Decorations 4 · 2.2 Line Patterns: the border-style properties

border-style: the ten styles

These properties control whether a border appears, and if it does what style it's drawn in (if it is not overridden by a border image). CSS Borders and Box Decorations 4 §2.2

Each style, 9px wide, in green

double: Two parallel solid lines with some space between them. (The thickness of the lines is not specified, but the sum of the lines and the space must equal 'border-width'.)

In front of the background, behind the content

Borders are drawn in front of the element's background, but behind the element's content (in case it overlaps).
