CSS Borders and Box Decorations 4 · 4.3 Drawing Areas: the border-image-width property

border-image-width: the regions

The four values of 'border-image-width' specify offsets that are used to divide the border image area into nine regions. The offsets represent inward distances from the top, right, bottom, and left sides of the area, respectively. CSS Borders and Box Decorations 4 §4.3 Each box is 120 by 60 with a 10px border, and the image of orange corners, blue dots and a green center, sliced at 10 with fill.

Numbers, lengths, percentages, auto

Numbers represent multiples of the corresponding computed border-width. If auto is specified then the used 'border-image-width' is the natural width or height (whichever is applicable) of the corresponding image slice

auto, and widths that overlap

If two opposite 'border-image-width' offsets are large enough that they overlap, then the used values of all 'border-image-width' offsets are proportionally reduced until they no longer overlap.
