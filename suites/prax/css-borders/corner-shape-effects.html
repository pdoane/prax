CSS Borders and Box Decorations 4 · 3.7 Corner Shaping: the corner-*-shape properties, effects

corner-shape: the border, clipping and shadows

The curve specified by 'corner-*-shape' defines the outer edge of the border. The inner edge of the border follows the curve of the outer edge with a nearly consistent distance from the outer edge throughout CSS Borders and Box Decorations 4 §3.7

The border, and no radius

Each box is 120 by 60, with border-radius: 30px unless said.

Clipping and shadows

elements are still clipped by the shaped border as normal. 'corner-*-shape' also affects the rendering of 'box-shadow'
