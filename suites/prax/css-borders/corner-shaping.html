Corner shaping: the inner curves

The padding edge (inner border) radius is the outer border radius minus the corresponding border thickness. In the case where this results in a negative value, the inner radius is zero. Likewise the content edge radius is the padding edge radius minus the corresponding padding, or if that is negative, zero. Thicknesses interpolated round the curve

when two adjoining borders are of different thicknesses the corner will show a smooth transition between the thicker and thinner borders. All border styles (solid, dotted, inset, etc.) follow the curve of the border.
