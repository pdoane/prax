box-shadow: painting order and overflow

the outer box-shadows of an element are drawn immediately below the background of that element, and the inner shadows of an element are drawn immediately above the background of that element (below the borders and border image, if any). CSS Borders and Box Decorations 4 §6.7

Between the background and the border

Each box has an orange background and a translucent blue 6px border.

No scrolling, and not on collapsed cells

Shadows do not trigger scrolling or increase the size of the scrollable area. Outer shadows have no effect on internal table elements in the collapsing border model.
