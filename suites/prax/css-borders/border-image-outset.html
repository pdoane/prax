border-image-outset: beyond the border box

The values specify the amount by which the border image area extends beyond the border box. CSS Borders and Box Decorations 4 §4.4 Each box is 100 by 40 with a 10px border, its light background showing where it is, and the image of orange corners and blue dots.

Lengths and multiples of the border width

<number [0,∞]>: Represents an outset of the specified multiple of the corresponding computed border-width.

Ink overflow

Portions of the border-image that are rendered outside the border box are ink overflow and do not trigger scrolling.
