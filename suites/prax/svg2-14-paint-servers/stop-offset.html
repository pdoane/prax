SVG 2 · 14.2.4.1 Attributes

stop: the offset

The ‘offset’ attribute Indicates were the gradient stop is placed. SVG 2 §14.2.4.1 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60 with a rect 120 by 40 at 20, 10.

Stops along the vector

The vector of colors to use in a gradient is defined by the ‘stop’ elements §14.2.4. For linear gradients, the ‘offset’ attribute represents a location along the gradient vector. It is a <number> or a <percentage>.

For a radial gradient, from the inner circle

For radial gradients, it represents a fractional distance from the edge of the innermost/smallest circle to the edge of the outermost/largest circle.
