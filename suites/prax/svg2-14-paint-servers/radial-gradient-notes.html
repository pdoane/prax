SVG 2 · 14.2.3.2 Notes on radial gradients

radialGradient: a start circle outside, or on the edge

If the start circle defined by ‘fx’, ‘fy’ and ‘fr’ lies outside the end circle defined by ‘cx’, ‘cy’, and ‘r’, effectively a cone is created, touched by the two circles. Areas outside the cone stay untouched by the gradient (transparent black). SVG 2 §14.2.3.2 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60 with a rect 120 by 40 at 20, 10; dashed circles mark the start and end circles.

Outside, covering, or on the edge

If the start circle fully overlaps with the end circle, no gradient is drawn. The area stays untouched (transparent black). Blue at the start circle, orange at the end.
