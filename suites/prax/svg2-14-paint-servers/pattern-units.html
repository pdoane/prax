SVG 2 · 14.3.1 Attributes

pattern: patternUnits and patternContentUnits

patternUnits Defines the coordinate system for attributes ‘x’, ‘y’, ‘width’ and ‘height’. patternContentUnits Defines the coordinate system for the contents of the ‘pattern’. SVG 2 §14.3.1 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60. Two rects, 60 by 40 at 10, 10 and 90, 10, take each pattern.

patternUnits: objectBoundingBox, the initial value, or userSpaceOnUse

A blue dot of radius 8 at 10, 10 in each tile.

patternContentUnits: userSpaceOnUse, the initial value, or objectBoundingBox

Tiles a quarter of each box wide and half tall, 15 by 20. Note that this attribute has no effect if attribute ‘viewBox’ is specified.
