CSS Color 4 · 13.4 Interpolating with Alpha

Interpolating with alpha: premultiplied

When the colors to be interpolated are not fully opaque, they are first premultiplied: For rectangular orthogonal color coordinate systems, all component values are multiplied by the alpha value. For cylindrical polar color coordinate systems, the hue angle is not premultiplied. CSS Color 4 §13.4

To and from transparent

A note: With premultiplied colors, transitions to or from "transparent" always look nice; unpremultiplied, the center of the gradient would be a noticeably grayish color. The bar is linear-gradient(90deg, red, transparent, blue) (CSS Images 4 §3.1) over white.

The specification's examples

rgb(24% 12% 98% / 0.4) and rgb(62% 26% 64% / 0.6) would first be converted to premultiplied form [9.6% 4.8% 39.2%] and [37.2% 15.6% 38.4%]; the midpoint, with alpha 0.5, undone, is rgb(46.8% 20.4% 77.6% / 0.5). In LCH, the L and C coordinates (but not H) are premultiplied, and rgb(76% 62% 3% / 0.4) to color(display-p3 0.84 0.19 0.72 / 0.6) has its midpoint at lch(58.873% 81.126 31.82 / 0.5).
