CSS Color 4 · 7 HSL Colors: hsl() and hsla() functions

hsl() and hsla()

HSL colors are specified as a triplet of hue, saturation, and lightness. HSL colors resolve to sRGB. CSS Color 4 §7 Each pair here is the HSL color, then the rgb() it converts to.

Hue, saturation and lightness

For saturation, 100% or 100 is a fully-saturated, bright color, and 0% or 0 is a fully-unsaturated gray. For lightness, 50% or 50 represents the "normal" color, while 100% or 100 is white and 0% or 0 is black. For historical reasons, hsl() and hsla() also support a legacy color syntax.
