CSS Color 4 · 13.5.1 shorter

Hue interpolation: shorter

Hue angles are interpolated to take the shorter of the two arcs between the starting and ending hues. It is the default. CSS Color 4 §13.5.1 Bars are linear-gradient(in oklch shorter hue to right, A, B) (CSS Images 4 §3.1).

Red to yellow, by orange

From a red, oklch(0.6 0.24 30), to a yellow, oklch(0.8 0.15 90): the midpoint [...] would be at a hue angle of 30 + (90 - 30) * 0.5 = 60 degrees, along the shorter arc between the two colors, giving a deep orange.

Across 0

From hue 350 to hue 10: Angles are adjusted so that |θ₂ - θ₁| ∈ [-180, 180], so the path crosses 0, 20 degrees long.
