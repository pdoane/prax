CSS Color 4 · 9.3 Specifying Lab and LCH: the lab() and lch() functional notations

lab() and lch()

CSS allows colors to be directly expressed in Lab and LCH. CSS Color 4 §9.3 Each pair is the specification's example, then the rgb() it converts to.

lab() and lch()

the first argument specifies the CIE Lightness, L. The second and third arguments are the distances along the "a" and "b" axes. In lch(), The second argument is the chroma C, The third argument is the hue angle H.

Lightness 0% and 100%

If the lightness of a Lab color (after clamping) is 0%, or 100% the color will be displayed as black, or white, respectively due to gamut mapping to the display. values greater than 100% or 100 are clamped to 100% at parsed-value time.
