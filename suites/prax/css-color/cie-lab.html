CIE Lab and LCH

The section is not normative. Lab is a rectangular coordinate system with a central Lightness (L) axis. CIE LCH has the same L axis as Lab, but uses polar coordinates C (chroma) and H (hue). CSS Color 4 §9.1

Lightness, and the a and b axes

equal increments in L are evenly spaced visually; positive values along the a axis are a purplish red while negative values are the complementary color, a green. Similarly, positive values along the b axis are yellow and negative are the complementary blue/violet.
