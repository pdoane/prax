Oklab and OkLCh

The section is not normative. Oklab has improved hue linearity, hue uniformity, and chroma uniformity compared to CIE LCH. CSS Color 4 §9.2

Lightness from 0 to 1

L=0% or 0.0 is deep black (no light at all) while L=100% or 1.0 is a diffuse white.
