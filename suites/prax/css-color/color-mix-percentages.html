CSS Color 5 · 3.2 Percentage Normalization

color-mix(): percentages

Percentages are required to be in the range 0% to 100%. Percentages are normalized by normalizing mix percentages. CSS Color 5 §3.2

Forms that are the same mix

These syntactic forms are thus all equivalent: purple and plum at 50% and 50%, at 50% and omitted, omitted and 50%, both omitted, in the other order, and at 80% and 80%, in lch. All produce a 50-50 mix, lch(51.51% 52.21 325.8).

Less than 100%: transparency

However, this form is not the same, as the alpha is less than one: color-mix(in lch, purple 30%, plum 30%) produces lch(51.51% 52.21 325.8 / 0.6). On a checkerboard.
