CSS Text 4 · 9.2.1 Hanging Punctuation: the hanging-punctuation property

hanging-punctuation: force-end and allow-end

force-end: A stop or comma at the end of a line hangs. allow-end: A stop or comma at the end of a line conditionally hangs. CSS Text 4 §9.2.1 The specification's example: text-align: justify, Japanese at 20px, eight to a 160px line. The text is highlighted.

A stop that fits

The first line is seven characters and "。", which fits. The punctuation at the end of the first line for 'allow-end' does not hang, because it fits without hanging. However, if 'force-end' is used, it is forced to hang. The justification measures the line without the hanging punctuation. Therefore when the line is expanded, the punctuation is pushed outside the line.

A stop that fits only hanging

Eight characters and "。": the stop goes on the first line only if it hangs.
