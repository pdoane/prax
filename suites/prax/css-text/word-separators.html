CSS Text 4 · 8.1 Word Spacing: the word-spacing property

Word separators

Word-separator characters are typographic character units whose primary purpose and general usage is to separate words. In Unicode this includes (but is not exhaustively defined as) the space (U+0020), the no-break space (U+00A0), the Ethiopic word space (U+1361), the Aegean word separators (U+10100,U+10101), the Ugaritic word divider (U+1039F), and the Phoenician word separator (U+1091F). CSS Text 4 §8.1 Each stage has word-spacing: 20px; the words are highlighted, so the added space shows as a gap.

Separators

Not separators

Neither punctuation in general, nor fixed-width spaces (such as U+3000 and U+2000 through U+200A), are considered word-separator characters.
