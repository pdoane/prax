CSS Text 4 · 6.2 Line Breaking Strictness: the line-break property

line-break: strict, normal, loose

This property specifies the strictness of line-breaking rules applied within an element: especially how wrapping interacts with punctuation and symbols. for these three keywords, this specification does require that: CSS Text 4 §6.2 Each stage is lang=ja, 100px wide, five 20px characters to a line; the sixth, blue, is the one the rule is about. Appendix F has the other writing systems.

Small kana

The following breaks are forbidden for 'normal' and 'strict' line breaking and allowed in 'loose': breaks before Japanese small kana or the Katakana-Hiragana prolonged sound mark, i.e. characters from the Unicode line breaking class CJ. normal forbids them since csswg-drafts#10363. The stages are あいうえお, small っ, か.

The wave dash

The following breaks are allowed for 'normal' and 'loose' line breaking if the writing system is Chinese or Japanese, and are otherwise forbidden: breaks before certain CJK hyphen-like characters: 〜 U+301C, ゠ U+30A0
