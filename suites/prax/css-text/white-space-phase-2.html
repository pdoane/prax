CSS Text 4 · 4.3.2 Phase II: Trimming and Positioning

Phase II: the ends of lines

A sequence of collapsible spaces at the end of a line is removed, as well as any trailing U+1680 OGHAM SPACE MARK whose 'white-space-collapse' property is 'collapse' or 'preserve-breaks'. CSS Text 4 §4.3.2 Each stage is 100px wide and right-aligned, so what is left at a line's end shows as a gap at the right. The text is highlighted.

The Ogham space mark

Ogham's word separator, U+1680, is drawn as a line along the stem. Each stage is two Ogham words, the mark, then a <br>.

Other space separators hang

If there remains any sequence of white space, other space separators, and/or preserved tabs at the end of a line (after bidi reordering): If 'white-space-collapse' is 'collapse' or 'preserve-breaks', the UA must hang this sequence (unconditionally). Letters are 10px wide.
