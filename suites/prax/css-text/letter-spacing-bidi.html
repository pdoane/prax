CSS Text 4 · 8.2 Tracking: the letter-spacing property

letter-spacing: between inlines, and after bidi

Letter-spacing is applied to each typographic character unit by inserting half of the additional spacing on each side; except it is not applied at the beginning or end of a line. Letter-spacing is applied after bidi reordering CSS Text 4 §8.2 The specification's examples, at 10px letters with letter-spacing: 10px on the stage and 20px on the blue span.

The average between two inlines

When the value varies between adjacent inlines, the effective spacing between adjacent letters of different inlines amounts to the average between the two. The stages are aa<span>bb</span>cc.

The line's ends after reordering

The specification's "ab", a span of "c" and alef, then bet and gimel: the Hebrew is reordered, so alef, in the span, is drawn last, at the line's end.
