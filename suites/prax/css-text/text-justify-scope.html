CSS Text 4 · 7.5 Justification Method: the text-justify property

Whose text-justify

whether a typographic character unit provides a justification opportunity is controlled by the 'text-justify' value of its parent; similarly, whether a justification opportunity exists between two consecutive typographic character units is determined by the 'text-justify' value of their nearest common ancestor. CSS Text 4 §7.5.1 Each stage is 200px wide, text-align: justify; its first line is justified, its second holds "seventeens". Letters are 10px wide; spans of text-justify: none are blue.

A word separator: its parent's value

"one two six", 90px to share between the two spaces.

Between characters: their nearest common ancestor

Each stage is text-justify: inter-character, and its first line "abcd ef", 130px wide, of 70px of letters.
