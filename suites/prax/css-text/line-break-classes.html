CSS Text 4 · 6.5 Line Breaking Details

Line breaking: Unicode's classes, boxes and priorities

Except where explicitly defined otherwise (e.g. for 'line-break: anywhere' or 'overflow-wrap: anywhere') line breaking behavior defined for the CM, and SG, WJ, ZW, GL, and ZWJ Unicode line breaking classes must be honored. Glue and joiners

Boxes do not break the text; priorities

Out-of-flow boxes and inline box boundaries do not introduce a forced line break or soft wrap opportunity in the flow. A break at a box's first or last character occurs immediately before/after the box (at its margin edge). UAs that break at punctuation should prioritize breakpoints, so that “check /etc” will never break between the "/" and the "e".
