CSS Text 4 · 7.6 Aligning a block of text within its container: the text-group-align property

text-group-align

Group alignment is performed by finding the line box with the shortest remaining space and adding that amount of space as padding to one or both sides of the line box, reducing the amount of space available for its contents; text alignment is then applied to its contents within the remaining space. CSS Text 4 §7.6 No browser has the property. Each stage holds two lines, a 120px box and a 60px one; the shortest remaining space is 80px.
