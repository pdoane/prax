For example, a right angle is 90deg or 100grad or 0.25turn or approximately 1.57rad. CSS Values and Units 4 §7.1

A right angle, four ways

when an angle denotes a direction in CSS, it is typically interpreted as a bearing angle, where 0deg is "up" or "north" on the screen, and larger angles are more clockwise (so 90deg is "right" or "east"). Each stage is a linear-gradient() from blue to orange at the angle under it.

An eighth of a turn

Each bar is rotate()d clockwise by the angle under it.
