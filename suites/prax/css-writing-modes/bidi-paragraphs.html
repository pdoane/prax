CSS Writing Modes 4 · 2.4 Applying the Bidirectional Reordering Algorithm

Bidi paragraphs

User agents that support bidirectional text must apply the Unicode bidirectional algorithm to every sequence of inline-level boxes uninterrupted by any block boundary or “bidi type B” forced paragraph break. This sequence forms the paragraph unit in the bidirectional algorithm. CSS Writing Modes 4 §2.4

One paragraph

Each stage is ltr and holds "abc", the Hebrew word אבג, "!", and the Hebrew word דהו. In one paragraph the "!" sits between two right-to-left words, and goes with them.

Paragraphs end at forced breaks and blocks

Ending the paragraph after the "!" leaves it between a right-to-left word and the paragraph's end, whose direction is ltr: it stays at the right of אבג.
