Vertical scripts in Unicode

This appendix lists the vertical-only and bi-orientational scripts in Unicode 6.0 [UNICODE] and their transformation from horizontal to vertical orientation. Any script not listed explicitly is assumed to be horizontal-only. The appendix is informative. CSS Writing Modes 4 §A

The listed transforms, and exceptions by width

Bopomofo, Hiragana, Katakana, Han and Hangul are listed with 0° and ttb: upright, top to bottom. Mongolian with 90°, Ogham with -90° and btt. Each stage is mixed.

For the purposes of this specification, all fullwidth (F) and wide (W) characters are treated as belonging to a vertical script, and halfwidth characters (H) are treated as belonging to a horizontal script.
