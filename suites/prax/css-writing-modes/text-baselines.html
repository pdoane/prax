CSS Writing Modes 4 · 4.2 Text Baselines

Text baselines: alphabetic and central

In vertical typographic mode, the central baseline is used as the dominant baseline when text-orientation is mixed or upright. Otherwise the alphabetic baseline is used. CSS Writing Modes 4 §4.2

Central in mixed and upright, alphabetic otherwise

The central baseline, which typically crosses the center of the em box; the alphabetic baseline, which typically aligns with the bottom of uppercase Latin glyphs. Each stage holds "Ap" in 16px and 32px Latin and "日本" in Japanese, on 50px lines; the dashed line is aligned to the line's dominant baseline.
