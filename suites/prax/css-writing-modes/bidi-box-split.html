CSS Writing Modes 4 · 2.4.5 Reordering-induced Box Fragmentation

Inline boxes split by reordering

Since bidi reordering can split apart and reorder text that is logically contiguous, bidirectional text can cause an inline box containing such text to be split and its fragments reordered within a line. CSS Writing Modes 4 §2.4.5

Split by content outside the box

The specification's example, <p>here is <em>some MIXED</em> TEXT.</p>, with the Hebrew word עברית for MIXED and טקסט for TEXT. The tinted em holds "some" and עברית; the two Hebrew words read from the right as one run, so טקסט lands between the em's two parts.

Reordered within the box

Content of one direction inside a box is reordered among itself; the box is not split where nothing outside it comes between.
