CSS Writing Modes 4 · 4.3 Atomic Inline Baselines

Baselines of atomic inlines

If an atomic inline (such as an inline-block, inline-table, or replaced inline element) does not have a baseline, then the UA synthesizes a baseline table. CSS Writing Modes 4 §4.3

Central: the middle of the margin box

The central baseline is assumed to be halfway between the under and over margin edges of the box. Each vertical-rl line holds a purple box 10 by 30; the dashed line is its central baseline.

Alphabetic: the under margin edge

The alphabetic baseline is assumed to be at the under margin edge. Under is the bottom of a horizontal line and the left of a vertical-rl one; the dashed line is the alphabetic baseline.
