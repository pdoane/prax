CSS Writing Modes 4 · 5 Introduction to Vertical Text Layout

Rotate or translate: scripts in vertical text

To lay out vertical text, the UA needs to transform the text from its horizontal orientation. This transformation is the bi-orientational transform, and there are two types: Rotate the glyph from horizontal to vertical and Translate the glyph from horizontal to vertical. CSS Writing Modes 4 §5

Vertical scripts carry their own transform

most CJK (Chinese/Japanese/Korean) characters translate, that is, they are always upright. Characters from other scripts, such as Mongolian, rotate.

Horizontal-only scripts: a choice

Scripts without a native vertical orientation can be either rotated (set sideways) or translated (set upright): the transform used is a stylistic preference; The text-orientation property's mixed and upright values are provided to specify rotation vs. translation of horizontal-only text.
