CSS Writing Modes 4 · 4.1 Introduction to Baselines

Baselines

A baseline is a line along the inline axis of a line box along which individual glyphs of text are aligned. The section is non-normative. CSS Writing Modes 4 §4.1

Two sizes on one baseline

the bottom of most alphabetic glyphs typically align with the alphabetic baseline. The dashed line is the line's baseline.

Horizontal and vertical tables

Different baseline tables can be provided for alignment in horizontal and vertical text. UAs should use the vertical tables in vertical typographic modes and the horizontal tables otherwise. The dashed lines mark the line's dominant baseline.
