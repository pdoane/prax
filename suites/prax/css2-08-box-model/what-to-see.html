CSS 2 · 8.2 Example of margins, padding, and borders

The example: what to see

A narrower containing block

What to see

  • The content width for each LI box is calculated top-down; the containing block for each LI box is established by the UL element. Each item is the list's content width less its 12px margins.
  • The margin box height of each LI box depends on its content height, plus top and bottom padding, borders, and margins. Note that vertical margins between the LI boxes collapse. The items are 12px apart, not 24px.
  • The list's 3px padding keeps the first item's top margin and the last item's bottom margin inside the list: yellow shows 3 + 12 = 15px above the first item and below the last.
  • The right padding of the LI boxes has been set to zero width. The text of the second item runs to its right border.
  • The margins of the LI boxes are transparent — margins are always transparent — so the background color (yellow) of the UL padding and content areas shines through them.
  • The second LI element specifies a dashed border. It is lime, of the medium width, on all four sides.
