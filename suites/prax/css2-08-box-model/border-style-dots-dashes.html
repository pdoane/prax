CSS 2 · 8.5.3 Border style

Border style: round dots and square-ended dashes

CSS 2 asks only for a series of dots and a series of short line segments. A later specification gives the dots and dashes their shapes. Every figure's border is 10px wide.

Since CSS 2 · CSS Borders and Box Decorations 4 §2.2

Round dots, square-ended dashes

dotted is A series of round dots. and dashed is A series of square-ended dashes. Their spacing is still the user agent's: There is no control over the spacing of the dots and dashes, nor over the length of the dashes. Implementations are encouraged to choose a spacing that makes the corners symmetrical. CSS Borders and Box Decorations 4 §2.2
