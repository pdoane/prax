CSS 2 · 8.3.1 Collapsing margins

Collapsing through a box

If the top and bottom margins of a box are adjoining, then it is possible for margins to collapse through it. A dashed outline is such a box. Every named box is 20px tall; the guides are 10px apart.

When a box's own margins collapse

A box's own margins collapse if the 'min-height' property is zero, and it has neither top or bottom borders nor top or bottom padding, and it has a 'height' of either 0 or 'auto', and it does not contain a line box, and all of its in-flow children's margins (if any) collapse.
