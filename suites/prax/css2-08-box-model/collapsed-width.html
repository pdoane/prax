CSS 2 · 8.3.1 Collapsing margins

The width of a collapsed margin

When two or more margins collapse, the resulting margin width is the maximum of the collapsing margins' widths. Negative margins: the largest of them is deducted from the largest positive one, or from zero. CSS 2 §8.3.1
