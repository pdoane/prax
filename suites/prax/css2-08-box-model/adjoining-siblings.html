CSS 2 · 8.3.1 Collapsing margins

Adjoining margins: siblings

In CSS, the adjoining margins of two or more boxes (which might or might not be siblings) can combine to form a single margin. Adjoining vertical margins collapse; this page shows which margins adjoin. Every box is 20px tall and the guides are 10px apart.

A box and its next sibling

The bottom margin of box and top margin of its next in-flow following sibling adjoin. CSS 2 §8.3.1

Horizontal margins

Horizontal margins never collapse. CSS 2 §8.3.1
