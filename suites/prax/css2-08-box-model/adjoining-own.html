CSS 2 · 8.3.1 Collapsing margins

Adjoining margins: a box's own

Adjoining vertical margins collapse into one. Every box is 20px tall and the guides are 10px apart. A dashed outline is a parent, which has no border or padding.

A box's own top and bottom

The top and bottom margins of a box that does not establish a new block formatting context and that has zero computed 'min-height', zero or 'auto' computed 'height', and no in-flow children adjoin. Margins collapse through such a box. CSS 2 §8.3.1
