CSS 2 · 8.5.3 Border style

Border style: double and the background

border-*-style and border-style take none | hidden | dotted | dashed | solid | double | groove | ridge | inset | outset.

double

Two lines and the space between them make up the width. CSS 2 §8.5.3

Borders are drawn over the background

All borders are drawn on top of the box's background. The box's background runs under its border, and shows in the gaps of a dotted, dashed or double border. CSS 2 §8.5.3
