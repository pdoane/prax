CSS 2 · 8.3 Margin properties

Margins: auto

margin-top, -right, -bottom, -left and margin take <length> | <percentage> | auto. The orange tint is each box's margin area.

auto

See the section on calculating widths and heights for behavior of 'auto'. For a block in normal flow, horizontal auto margins take up the room the width leaves, and vertical auto margins are 0. CSS 2 §10.3.3, §10.6.3
