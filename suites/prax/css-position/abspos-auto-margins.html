CSS Positioned Layout 3 · 4.2 Auto Margins of Absolutely-Positioned Boxes

Auto margins of absolutely positioned boxes

If either inset property in the relevant axis is auto, then any auto margins resolve to zero. Otherwise, the remaining space is calculated as the size of its inset-modified containing block in the relevant axis minus the box's used size in the relevant axis, and this remaining space is divided among any auto margins in the relevant axis.
