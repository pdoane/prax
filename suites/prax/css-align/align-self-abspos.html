align-self on absolutely positioned boxes

The alignment container is The box's containing block, as modified by the inset properties ('top'/'right'/'bottom'/'left'), assuming the writing mode of the containing block. Fit-content heights, aligned

Values other than stretch or normal cause non-replaced absolutely-positioned boxes to use fit-content sizing for calculating auto sizes in the affected axis.

Replaced boxes, and one auto inset

stretch does cause replaced absolutely-positioned boxes to fill their containing block. If only one inset property is auto, 'align-self' has no effect. The image is 40 by 20.
