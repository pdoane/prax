CSS Box Alignment 3 · 5.3 Alignment Overflow and Scroll Containers

Alignment in scroll containers

When the content-distribution properties are set in a scrollable axis of a scroll container with an overflowing alignment subject, they reduce the clipping of the unreachable scrollable overflow region just enough to ensure that alignment subject can be scrolled into its start-aligned position.
