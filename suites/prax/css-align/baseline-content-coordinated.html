CSS Box Alignment 3 · 5.4 Baseline Content-Alignment, participation

Baseline content-alignment: who takes part

in order to participate in baseline content-alignment it must also have a coordinated self-alignment preference, to guarantee that the box lines up the relevant edge with other boxes in its baseline-sharing group. It otherwise takes its fallback alignment. CSS Box Alignment 3 §5.4

A coordinated self-alignment preference

For “first”, the relevant self-alignment property either is or aligns identically to stretch or self-start. A grid row 60px tall; all items align-content: baseline, the third 30px tall.

Table cells: the startmost or endmost row

A (non-replaced) table cell participates in first/last baseline content-alignment in its startmost/endmost row. Rows are 40px; A spans both, at 32px; C, in the second row, has two lines.
