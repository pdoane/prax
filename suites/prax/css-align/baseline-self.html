CSS Box Alignment 3 · 6.4 Baseline Self-Alignment

Baseline self-alignment

Baseline self-alignment effectively increases the margins on the box to align its alignment baseline with that of other baseline-aligned boxes in its baseline-sharing group. CSS Box Alignment 3 §6.4 Items are highlighted; "Ag" at 13 and 32px.

Flex items, and grid items in a row

A flex item in its flex line; a grid item in its startmost/endmost row or column, by align-self for a row.

Grid items in a column: justify-self

By justify-self, items share the grid’s column (block) axis. The two items, in one 200px column, are vertical-rl with sideways text, so their baselines run down the page.
