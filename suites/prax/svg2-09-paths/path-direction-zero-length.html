SVG 2 · 9.4 Path directionality

The direction at zero length

Some features, such as the orientation of markers and the shapes of line caps, are defined in terms of the direction of the path. SVG 2 §9.4 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60. The orange arrows are markers 16 wide with orient="auto", at an end pointing in the path direction §13.7.4.

A path of zero length, and a subpath

If length of the entire path the segment belongs to is zero, then the direction at the start of the segment points in the same direction as the positive x-axis. A square cap is oriented such that two of its sides are parallel to the effective tangent §13.5.4.
