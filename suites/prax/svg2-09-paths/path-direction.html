SVG 2 · 9.4 Path directionality

The direction of a path

Some features, such as the orientation of markers and the shapes of line caps, are defined in terms of the direction of the path. SVG 2 §9.4 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60. The orange arrows are markers 16 wide with orient="auto", at an end pointing in the path direction §13.7.4.

At the start and end of a path

If the given distance is zero, then the direction of the path is the direction at the start of the path's first segment. A zero-length segment takes its direction from the closest preceding non-zero length segment, or, with none before it, from the closest following non-zero length segment.
