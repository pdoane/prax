SVG 2 · 8.7 The ‘preserveAspectRatio’ attribute

preserveAspectRatio: align

The <align> parameter indicates whether to force uniform scaling and, if so, the alignment method to use in case the aspect ratio of the ‘viewBox’ doesn't match the aspect ratio of the SVG viewport. SVG 2 §8.7 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60. The content is a blue rect as large as the viewBox with an orange dot at its middle.

xMin, xMid, xMax

Align the <min-x> of the element's ‘viewBox’ with the smallest X value of the SVG viewport. viewBox="0 0 20 20", scaled 3 to 60 by 60.

YMin, YMid, YMax

viewBox="0 0 80 10", scaled 2 to 160 by 20.
