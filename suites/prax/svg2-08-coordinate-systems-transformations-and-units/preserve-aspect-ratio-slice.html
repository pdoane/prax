SVG 2 · 8.7 The ‘preserveAspectRatio’ attribute

preserveAspectRatio: meet, slice and none

The <meetOrSlice> parameter is optional. SVG 2 §8.7 Each stage is an SVG 160 by 60 with viewBox="0 0 20 20". The content, as large as the viewBox, is blue with a green band across its middle half and an orange dot at its center.

slice

the entire SVG viewport is covered by the ‘viewBox’; the ‘viewBox’ is scaled down as much as possible, while still meeting the other criteria. Scaled 8, to 160 by 160.

none, and no viewBox

none: Scale the graphic content of the given element non-uniformly if necessary. if attribute ‘viewBox’ is not provided, then ‘preserveAspectRatio’ is ignored.
