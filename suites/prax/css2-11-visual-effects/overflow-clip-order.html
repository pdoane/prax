CSS 2 · 11.1.1 Overflow: the 'overflow' property

The overflow property: clipping out of order

Each stage is position: relative; z-index: 0, a stacking context. Each dashed box is 40 by 40 with overflow: hidden, and holds a blue box with z-index: -1, 120px wide at top: 0; left: 0.

Painted out of order, clipped in order

A box at z-index: -1 is painted before the content around it, and yet clipped as its place in the tree says: the overflow clips its descendants, except any descendant elements ... whose containing block is the viewport or an ancestor of the element. CSS 2 §11.1.1
