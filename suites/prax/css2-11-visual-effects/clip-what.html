CSS 2 · 11.1.2 Clipping: the 'clip' property

The clip property: what it clips

A clipping region cuts all that the element draws, and its ancestors' clipping adds to it.

Every part of the element

An element's clipping region clips out any aspect of the element (e.g., content, children, background, borders, text decoration, outline and visible scrolling mechanism — if any) that is outside the clipping region. CSS 2 §11.1.2

The cumulative intersection

The element's ancestors may also clip portions of their content (e.g., via their own 'clip' property and/or if their 'overflow' property is not 'visible'); what is rendered is the cumulative intersection. CSS 2 §11.1.2
