CSS 2 · 11.1.1 Overflow: the 'overflow' property

The overflow property: what it clips

A box's overflow clips the positioned descendants whose containing block it is or is inside of. Each dashed box has overflow: hidden, each stage is position: relative, and each blue box is 80px wide.

Not boxes positioned past it

It affects the clipping of all of the element's content except any descendant elements (and their respective content and descendants) whose containing block is the viewport or an ancestor of the element. CSS 2 §11.1.1

Through the ancestors in between

Absolutely positioned boxes are not clipped by the overflow of any ancestor between themselves and their containing block. The viewport is the containing block of a fixed box. CSS 2 §11.1
