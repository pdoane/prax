CSS Fonts 4 · 6.10 East Asian text rendering: the 'font-variant-east-asian' property

font-variant-east-asian

'font-variant-east-asian' selects the glyph forms of a Japanese standard (jis78, jis83, jis90, jis04), simplified or traditional forms, full-width or proportional-width, and ruby forms. CSS Fonts 4 §6.10

JIS forms

PR Noto Sans JP has JIS78, JIS83 and JIS90 forms. 唖 and 噛 differ in JIS78; 倦 and 咬 in JIS90.
