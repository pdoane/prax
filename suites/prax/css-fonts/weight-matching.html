CSS Fonts 4 · 5.2 Matching font styles, step 4.3

Matching weight

Between 400 and 500, weights greater than or equal to the target weight are checked in ascending order until 500 is hit, then lighter, then heavier than 500. Below 400, lighter first; above 500, heavier first. CSS Fonts 4 §5.2

Faces at 300, 500 and 700

"Weights" has Tinos (serif) at 300, Cousine (monospace) at 500 and Arimo (sans-serif) at 700, each declared, so none is emboldened.

Faces at 300 and 600

"Gap" has Tinos at 300 and Arimo at 600.
