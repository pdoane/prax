CSS Fonts 4 · 6.1 Glyph selection and positioning

Glyph selection and positioning

In OpenType, The glyph for a given character can be chosen and positioned not just based on the codepoint of the character itself, but also on adjacent characters as well as the language, script, and features enabled for the text. The 'font-variant' subproperties affect glyph selection and positioning, but do not affect font selection. CSS Fonts 4 §6.1
