CSS Fonts 4 · 6.5 Subscript and superscript forms: the 'font-variant-position' property

font-variant-position

sub and super enable the font's subscript and superscript glyphs. if a variant glyph is not available for all the characters in the run, reduced forms of the usual glyphs are used for all of them, to avoid a mixture of variant glyphs and synthesized ones that would not align correctly.
