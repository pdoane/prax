CSS 2 · 14.2 The background

The background of a stacking context

Where an element's background is painted, relative to its descendants, depends on whether the element forms a stacking context. The painting order is in §9.9.1 and Appendix E.

At the bottom of its stacking context

Backgrounds of elements that form a stacking context (see the 'z-index' property) are painted at the bottom of the element's stacking context, below anything in that stacking context. CSS 2 §14.2
