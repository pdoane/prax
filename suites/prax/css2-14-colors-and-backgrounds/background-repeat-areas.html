CSS 2 · 14.2.1 Background properties

Background repeat: areas and the band

background-repeat takes repeat | repeat-x | repeat-y | no-repeat | inherit. The image is a 20px blue dot unless a figure says otherwise.

Tiles cover the border area

All tiling covers the content, padding and border areas of a box. CSS 2 §14.2.1

A box with a 10px brown dashed border and 10px padding, its image at the initial 0% 0%: the top left of its padding box, 10px inside the border edge.

A vertical band

The specification's example, background: white url("pendant.png"); background-repeat: repeat-y; background-position: center: One copy of the background image is centered, and other copies are put above and below it to make a vertical band behind the element. CSS 2 §14.2.1
