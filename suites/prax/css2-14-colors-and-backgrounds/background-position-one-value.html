CSS 2 · 14.2.1 Background properties

Background position: one value, and keyword order

background-position takes [ [ <percentage> | <length> | left | center | right ] [ <percentage> | <length> | top | center | bottom ]? ] | [ [ left | center | right ] || [ top | center | bottom ] ]. The image is a 50 by 30 banner in a box 200 by 100.

One value: the other is center

If only one value is specified, the second value is assumed to be 'center'. CSS 2 §14.2.1

Two keywords, in either order

With keywords alone the grammar is [ left | center | right ] || [ top | center | bottom ]: one of each, in either order. CSS 2 §14.2.1
