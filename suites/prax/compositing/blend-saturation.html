Compositing and Blending 2 · 10.2.2 saturation blend mode

saturation: the source's saturation

Creates a color with the saturation of the source color and the hue and luminosity of the backdrop color. Painting with this mode in an area of the backdrop that is a pure gray (no saturation) produces no change. Compositing and Blending 2 §10.2.2

B(Cb, Cs) = SetLum(SetSat(Cb, Sat(Cs)), Lum(Cb))

Rows, top to bottom: the backdrop, black, white, grey 50%, blue and orange; the source; mix-blend-mode; background-blend-mode over the backdrop as background-color; the formula's color and its hex.
