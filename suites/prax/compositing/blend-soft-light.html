Compositing and Blending 2 · 10.1.10 soft-light blend mode

soft-light: a diffused spotlight

Darkens or lightens the colors, depending on the source color value. The effect is similar to shining a diffused spotlight on the backdrop. Compositing and Blending 2 §10.1.10

The formula

Rows, top to bottom: the backdrop, black, white, grey 50%, blue and orange; the source; mix-blend-mode; background-blend-mode over the backdrop as background-color; the formula's color and its hex.

if(Cs <= 0.5)
    B(Cb, Cs) = Cb - (1 - 2 x Cs) x Cb x (1 - Cb)
else
    B(Cb, Cs) = Cb + (2 x Cs - 1) x (D(Cb) - Cb)
with
    if(Cb <= 0.25)
        D(Cb) = ((16 * Cb - 12) x Cb + 4) x Cb
    else
        D(Cb) = sqrt(Cb)

The two branches of D

A darker backdrop here: black, 32, 63, 64 and 128 grey, either side of 0.25, which is 63.75.
