Compositing and Blending 2 · 10.1.8 color-burn blend mode

color-burn: darkening the backdrop

Darkens the backdrop color to reflect the source color. Painting with white produces no change. Compositing and Blending 2 §10.1.8

The formula

Rows, top to bottom: the backdrop, black, white, grey 50%, blue and orange; the source; mix-blend-mode; background-blend-mode over the backdrop as background-color; the formula's color and its hex.

if(Cb == 1)
    B(Cb, Cs) = 1
else if(Cs == 0)
    B(Cb, Cs) = 0
else
    B(Cb, Cs) = 1 - min(1, (1 - Cb) / Cs)
