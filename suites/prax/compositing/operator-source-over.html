The source-over operator

With the source-over compositing operator, the source is placed over the destination. Compositing and Blending 2 §9.1.4 On white, the destination, blue rgb(0% 40% 80% / 0.8) at (10, 10), then the source, orange rgb(100% 60% 0% / 0.6) at (60, 30), each 90 by 60, the source with "source-over".

In a canvas

Fa = 1; Fb = 1 – αs
co = αs x Cs + αb x Cb x (1 – αs)
αo = αs + αb x (1 – αs)
