The plus-darker operator

With the plus-darker compositing operator, the following steps are performed: Compositing and Blending 2 §9.1.14 On a white canvas, the destination, blue rgb(0% 40% 80% / 0.8), is drawn first, 90 by 60 at (10, 10); the source, orange rgb(100% 60% 0% / 0.6), is drawn over it at (60, 30) with globalCompositeOperation = "plus-darker".

In a canvas

Fa = 1; Fb = 1
co = max(0, 1 - αs x Cs + 1 - αb x Cb);
αo = max(0, 1 - αs + 1 - αb);

As written, the formula gives no color to expect: where neither rectangle is, αo is max(0, 2), and in the overlap co's red is 1.4 against an αo of 0.6, more color than alpha. What plus-darker should draw is open in fxtf-drafts#447, which the working group resolved to define from research into what implementations draw.
