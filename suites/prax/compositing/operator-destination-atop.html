Compositing and Blending 2 · 9.1.11 Destination Atop

The destination-atop operator

With the destination-atop compositing operator, the parts of the destination which overlaps the source replace the source. The source is placed everywhere else. Compositing and Blending 2 §9.1.11 On white, the destination, blue rgb(0% 40% 80% / 0.8) at (10, 10), then the source, orange rgb(100% 60% 0% / 0.6) at (60, 30), each 90 by 60, the source with "destination-atop".
