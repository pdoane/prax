Compositing and Blending 2 · 10.1.9 hard-light blend mode

hard-light: multiply or screen by the source

Multiplies or screens the colors, depending on the source color value. The effect is similar to shining a harsh spotlight on the backdrop. Compositing and Blending 2 §10.1.9

The formula

Rows, top to bottom: the backdrop, black, white, grey 50%, blue and orange; the source; mix-blend-mode; background-blend-mode over the backdrop as background-color; the formula's color and its hex.

if(Cs <= 0.5)
    B(Cb, Cs) = Multiply(Cb, 2 x Cs)
else
    B(Cb, Cs) = Screen(Cb, 2 x Cs -1)
