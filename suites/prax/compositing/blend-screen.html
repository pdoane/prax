Compositing and Blending 2 · 10.1.3 screen blend mode

screen: the complement of the product

Multiplies the complements of the backdrop and source color values, then complements the result. Compositing and Blending 2 §10.1.3

The formula

Rows, top to bottom: the backdrop, black, white, grey 50%, blue and orange; the source; mix-blend-mode; background-blend-mode over the backdrop as background-color; the formula's color and its hex.

B(Cb, Cs) = 1 - [(1 - Cb) x (1 - Cs)]
          = Cb + Cs -(Cb x Cs)

White and black

Screening any color with white produces white; screening with black leaves the original color unchanged.
