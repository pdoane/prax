Compositing and Blending 2 · 10.2.4 luminosity blend mode

luminosity: the source's luminosity

Creates a color with the luminosity of the source color and the hue and saturation of the backdrop color. This produces an inverse effect to that of the Color mode. Compositing and Blending 2 §10.2.4

B(Cb, Cs) = SetLum(Cb, Lum(Cs))
