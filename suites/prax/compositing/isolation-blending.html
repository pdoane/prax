Compositing and Blending 2 · 10.3 Effect of group isolation on blending

Blending inside an isolated group

In the isolated group, the elements within the group are composited onto an empty initial backdrop, this stops the elements within the group multiplying with the backdrop. In the normal group, the elements within the group are composited onto the initial backdrop containing the land and sky. Compositing and Blending 2 §10.3

The section's example

Each stage is 200 by 80: sky, #b9cde6, above land, #6aa36a, split at 40. In a group are two 40px squares, orange, #d68a3c, from x 60, and blue, #4f81bd, from x 80 over it, both mix-blend-mode: multiply. Below each scene, the colors it should show. In both instances, the result of the group composite is composited onto the land and sky using the normal mix-blend-mode.
