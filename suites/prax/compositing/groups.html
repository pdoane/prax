Compositing and Blending 2 · 8 Compositing Groups

Compositing groups

A compositing group is rendered by first compositing the elements of the group onto the initial backdrop. The result of this is a single element containing color and alpha information. This element is then composited onto the group backdrop. Compositing and Blending 2 §8 Each stage has a lime band 140 by 40; the group holds a red square 50 wide at x 30 and a blue one at x 60, overlapping from x 60 to 80.

The initial backdrop

This will be the same as the group backdrop in a non-isolated group, or a fully transparent backdrop for an isolated group. Both squares have mix-blend-mode: multiply.

A single contribution

Steps shall be taken to ensure the group backdrop makes only a single contribution to the final composite. The group, opacity: 0.5, holds the opaque squares, normal.
