The Porter Duff operators

The fractional terms Fa (1 in this example) and Fb (1 – αs in this example) are defined for each operator and specify the fraction of the shapes that may contribute to the final pixel value. Compositing and Blending 2 §9.1 Each operator's page draws these two rectangles with it.

The source and the destination

Destination is synonymous with backdrop. §9 The destination is drawn first, the source over it.

The general equation

co = αs x Fa x Cs + αb x Fb x Cb
