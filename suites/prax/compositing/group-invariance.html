Compositing and Blending 2 · 8.1 Group invariance

Group invariance

Adding or removing grouping with default attributes shall not show visual differences. so: A + B + C = A + (B + C) = (A + B) + C Compositing and Blending 2 §8.1 Each stage holds red, lime and blue squares at 0.5, 60 by 40 at x 10, 50 and 90.

Three groupings, one picture

The groups are divs with default attributes: no stacking context, no blending.

Isolated, with normal blending

When adding attributes to the group such as isolate, blending modes other than normal or Porter Duff compositing operators other than source-over, groups may no longer be invariant. With source-over and normal blending alone, an isolated group is still the same.
