Compositing and Blending 2 · 10.1.6 lighten blend mode

lighten: the lighter of the two

Selects the lighter of the backdrop and source colors. Compositing and Blending 2 §10.1.6

B(Cb, Cs) = max(Cb, Cs)

Rows, top to bottom: the backdrop, black, white, grey 50%, blue and orange; the source; mix-blend-mode; background-blend-mode over the backdrop as background-color; the formula's color and its hex.

Channel by channel
