Compositing and Blending 2 · 3.1 Order of graphical operations

Filter, then clip, mask, blend and composite

The compositing model must follow the SVG compositing model: first any filter effect is applied, then any clipping, masking, blending and compositing. Compositing and Blending 2 §3.1 In each stage a red 40px square sits over a lime band 80 by 40; the square at the right is the color expected.

The filter comes before the clip

The square has filter: drop-shadow(20px 10px 0 purple). The shadow is part of what the filter draws, and the clip that follows cuts it away.

The filter comes before the blend

The square has filter: grayscale(1) and mix-blend-mode: multiply. Filtered first, red becomes the grey rgb(21% 21% 21%) (0.2126 of red); multiplied by lime, that is rgb(0% 21% 0%). Blended first, red times lime would be black, and grey still black.
