Compositing and Blending 2 · 10.2.3 color blend mode

color: the source's hue and saturation

Creates a color with the hue and saturation of the source color and the luminosity of the backdrop color. This preserves the gray levels of the backdrop and is useful for coloring monochrome images or tinting color images. Compositing and Blending 2 §10.2.3

B(Cb, Cs) = SetLum(Cs, Lum(Cb))
