HTML · 6.8.3 Best practices for in-page editors

Editing hosts and white-space

Authors are encouraged to set the 'white-space' property on editing hosts and on markup that was originally created through these editing mechanisms to the value 'pre-wrap'. HTML §6.8.3

Two spaces typed between two words

With normal, the resulting markup will either consist of "yellow&nbsp; ball" or "yellow &nbsp;ball". In the former case, "yellow⍽" might wrap to the next line; in the latter case, "⍽ball", if wrapped to the start of the line, would have visible indentation from the non-breaking space. When 'white-space' is set to 'pre-wrap', however, the editing rules will instead simply put two regular spaces between the words, and should the two words be split at the end of a line, the spaces would be neatly removed from the rendering. §6.8.3
