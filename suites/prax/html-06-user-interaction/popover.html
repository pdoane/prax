The popover attribute

With the popover attribute, the element won't be rendered until it becomes shown, at which point it will be rendered on top of other page content. HTML §6.12

Hidden, then on top, in the middle of the window

The Rendering section gives a popover not open display: none, and every popover position: fixed; inset: 0; width: fit-content; height: fit-content; margin: auto; border: solid; padding: 0.25em. §15.3.3

Auto closes other autos; manual does not

auto: Closes other popovers when opened; has light dismiss and responds to close requests. manual: Does not close other popovers; does not light dismiss or respond to close requests. §6.12
