CSS 2 · 17.5.1 Table layers and transparency

Table layers: the areas they cover

Each table is yellow, with transparent cells 40 by 20 and a thin border. A column or row's background covers its cells, including cells that start in it and span out of it.

Columns and rows cover the cells that start in them

A column's background covers exactly the full area of all cells that originate in the column, even if they span outside the column, but this difference in area does not affect background image positioning. For rows, As with columns, the background covers exactly the full area of all cells that originate in the row, even if they span outside the row. CSS 2 §17.5.1
