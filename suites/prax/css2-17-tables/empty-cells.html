CSS 2 · 17.6.1.1 Borders and Backgrounds around empty cells: the 'empty-cells' property

Empty cells: the empty-cells property

empty-cells takes show | hide. Initial show, inherited, and it applies to table-cell elements. Tables are yellow with border-spacing: 10px; cells are blue with a 2px black border.

show and hide

In the separated borders model, this property controls the rendering of borders and backgrounds around cells that have no visible content. show: borders and backgrounds are drawn around/behind empty cells (like normal cells). hide: no borders or backgrounds are drawn around/behind empty cells.
