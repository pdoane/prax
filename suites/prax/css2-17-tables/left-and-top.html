CSS 2 · 17.6.2.1 Border conflict resolution

Border conflicts: left and top

The last tie-breaker, and the introduction's open question. All tables collapse their borders.

4. Between elements of the same type

When two elements of the same type conflict, then the one further to the left (if the table's 'direction' is 'ltr'; right, if it is 'rtl') and further to the top wins. Orange and blue borders here are both 8px solid. CSS 2 §17.6.2.1

The introduction's rows

What color (black or blue) and thickness (1px or 3px) will the border between row1 and row2 be? Row 1 has a 3px solid blue border, rows 2 and 3 1px solid black ones. CSS 2 §17.1, §17.6.2.1
