Min-content and max-content constraints, and the preferred aspect ratio

A min-content constraint is A sizing constraint imposed by the box's containing block that causes it to produce its min-content contribution. CSS Box Sizing 3 §2.3

Laid out under a constraint

A preferred aspect ratio applies to the content box

Unless otherwise specified, a box’s preferred aspect ratio is its natural aspect ratio if it has one and is applied to its content box. Each image has a 2:1 ratio and no natural size, and 10px of green padding.
