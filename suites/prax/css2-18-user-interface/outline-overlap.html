CSS 2 · 18.4 Dynamic outlines: the 'outline' property

Outlines overlap other content

An outline is left out of layout, so nothing makes room for it: it is drawn over whatever is next to its box.

Outlines may overlap other content

Since the outline does not affect formatting (i.e., no space is left for it in the box model), it may well overlap other elements on the page. CSS 2 §18.4
