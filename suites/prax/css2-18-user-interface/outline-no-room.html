CSS 2 · 18.4 Dynamic outlines: the 'outline' property

Outlines take no room

Outlines do not take up space. Each pair compares a 10px border with a 10px outline on box A; box B follows A in the flow.

Drawn over, not laid out

The outline created with the outline properties is drawn "over" a box, i.e., the outline is always on top, and does not influence the position or size of the box, or of any other boxes. CSS 2 §18.4

Just outside the border edge, and no overflow

The outline may be drawn starting just outside the border edge. Therefore, displaying or suppressing outlines does not cause reflow or overflow. CSS 2 §18.4
