Resolving flexible lengths: factors below 1

Calculate the remaining free space as for initial free space, above. If the sum of the unfrozen flex items’ flex factors is less than one, multiply the initial free space by this sum. If the magnitude of this value is less than the magnitude of the remaining free space, use this as the remaining free space. CSS Flexible Box Layout 2 §9.8
