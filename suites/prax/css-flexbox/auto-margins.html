CSS Flexible Box Layout 2 · 8.1 Aligning with auto margins

auto margins take the free space

Auto margins on flex items have an effect very similar to auto margins in block flow. CSS Flexible Box Layout 2 §8.1

Positive free space goes to the auto margins

Prior to alignment via justify-content and align-self, any positive free space is distributed to auto margins in that dimension. Items 40px wide.

Zero while flexing, and they leave alignment nothing

During calculations of flex bases and flexible lengths, auto margins are treated as 0. If free space is distributed to auto margins, the alignment properties will have no effect in that dimension.
