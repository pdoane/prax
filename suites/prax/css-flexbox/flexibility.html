Flexibility: growing and shrinking

The defining aspect of flex layout is the ability to make the flex items “flex”, altering their width/height to fill the available space in the main dimension. CSS Flexible Box Layout 2 §7

Grow to fill, shrink to fit

A flex container distributes free space to its items (proportional to their flex grow factor) to fill the container, or shrinks them (proportional to their flex shrink factor) to prevent overflow. Each stage is a flex container 200px wide.

Fully inflexible items

A flex item is fully inflexible if both its flex-grow and flex-shrink values are zero, and flexible otherwise.
