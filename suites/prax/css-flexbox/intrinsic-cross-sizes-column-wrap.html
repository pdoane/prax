CSS Flexible Box Layout 2 · 9.10.2 Flex Container Intrinsic Cross Sizes, column multi-line

Intrinsic widths of column-wrap flex containers

For a column multi-line flex container, The max-content cross size is the sum of the flex line cross sizes resulting from sizing the flex container under a cross-axis max-content constraint, using the largest max-content cross-size contribution among the flex items as the available space in the cross axis for each of the flex items during layout. max-content: the sum of the columns

Each dashed container is column wrap, 40px tall, two 20px items to a line, of align-items: flex-start: "aaa", "bb", "cccc", "d" and "ee", 30, 20, 40, 10 and 20px wide.

min-content: the largest contribution

The min-content cross size is the largest min-content contribution among all of its flex items. The items are "aaa bb" and "c dddd": min-content contributions of 30 and 40px.
