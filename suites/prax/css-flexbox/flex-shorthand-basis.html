flex: a basis of 0, of 0% and of auto

The flex basis is the initial main size of the flex item, before free space is distributed according to the flex factors. CSS Flexible Box Layout 2 §7.1

“Absolute” and “relative” flex

The spec's figure: the difference between "absolute" flex (starting from a basis of zero) and "relative" flex (starting from a basis of the item's content size). The three items have flex factors of 1, 1, and 2, respectively. Their words are 20px, 40px and 20px wide.
