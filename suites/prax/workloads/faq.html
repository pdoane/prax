Orders

When will my order ship?

Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day leave the warehouse the same day. Larger items, such as shelving units, are sent by freight and leave within two working days.

Can I change the delivery address?

Until the order is packed, yes: open the order and choose a new address. After that, contact the carrier with the tracking number from your confirmation email.

Do you ship outside the EU?

We ship to Switzerland, Norway and the United Kingdom. Duties are charged on delivery.

What are the duties for the United Kingdom?

Orders under 135 GBP are charged VAT at checkout and nothing on delivery.

Returns

How long do I have to return an item?

Thirty days from delivery, unused and in its original packaging.

Who pays for the return shipping?

We do, for anything that arrived damaged or was not what you ordered. Otherwise the return label costs 6.90 EUR, which we take from your refund.
