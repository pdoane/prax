Painting order: negative stack levels

Step 3. Each stage forms the stacking context, with position: relative; z-index: 0, and its absolutely positioned boxes are in the source in the order A, B, C.

After the background, before the content

Stacking contexts formed by positioned descendants with negative z-indices (excluding 0) come after the context's own background and border (step 2) and before its blocks, floats and lines (steps 4 to 7). CSS 2 §E.2
