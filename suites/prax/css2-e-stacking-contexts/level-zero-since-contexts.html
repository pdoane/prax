CSS 2 · E.2 Painting order

Painting order: contexts that hold levels

Each dashed parent has z-index: auto and holds a blue child with z-index: 5; the orange sibling after it has z-index: 1. Where the parent forms a stacking context, the child's 5 counts only inside it, and the sibling covers the child.
