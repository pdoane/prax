Painting order: a positioned inline at level 0

Each stage forms the stacking context. On its line, an orange span of "abc" is followed by a blue inline block pulled 30px back over it.

Step 8, after the line

All positioned descendants with 'z-index: auto' or 'z-index: 0', in tree order. For those with 'z-index: auto', treat the element as if it created a new stacking context: a relatively positioned inline is painted whole, after the lines of step 7 and the inline blocks on them. CSS 2 §E.2
