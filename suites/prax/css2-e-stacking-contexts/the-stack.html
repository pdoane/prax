CSS 2 · E.2 Painting order

Painting order: the stack and the canvas

The bottom of the stack is the furthest from the user, the top of the stack is the nearest to the user: what is painted later is nearer, and covers what is painted earlier. CSS 2 §E.2

From the background to the most positive level

The stacking context background and most negative positioned stacking contexts are at the bottom of the stack, while the most positive positioned stacking contexts are at the top of the stack. CSS 2 §E.2

The canvas

The canvas is transparent if contained within another, and given a UA-defined color if it is not. It is infinite in extent and contains the root element. Initially, the viewport is anchored with its top left corner at the canvas origin. In each frame, neither root nor body has a background. CSS 2 §E.2
