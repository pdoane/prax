Painting order: notes

Two consequences of the painting order: for the root's background, and for the backgrounds of inlines in bidirectional text.

The root's background, once

The background of the root element is only painted once, over the whole canvas. In each frame, the root is a box 100 by 40 with a white dashed border. CSS 2 §E.3

Painted in tree order, placed in visual order

While the backgrounds of bidirectional inlines are painted in tree order, they are positioned in visual order. Since the positioning of inline backgrounds is unspecified in CSS 2.2, the exact result of these two requirements is UA-defined. CSS 2 §E.3
