Painting order: block-level descendants

Step 4 paints the backgrounds and borders of in-flow, non-positioned blocks, and nothing else of them; their lines come in step 7. Each figure's second block has a negative top margin that pulls it up over the first.

Every block's background before any line

For all its in-flow, non-positioned, block-level descendants in tree order: If the element is a block, list-item, or other block equivalent: background color of element. background image of element. border of element. CSS 2 §E.2

One block at a time, and only non-positioned ones
