# The cases of the bmpsuite suite whose answers differ from what the suite expects of a file, each with why. An entry is
# its keys, then its sentence on indented lines, and the entries are sorted by their first key.

g/rgb16-565.bmp
g/rgb16-565pal.bmp
g/rgb16.bmp
g/rgb16bfdef.bmp
q/rgb16-3103.bmp
q/rgb16faketrns.bmp
q/rgb32-111110.bmp
q/rgb32-7187.bmp
q/rgba16-5551.bmp
q/rgba32-1010102.bmp
q/rgba32-61754.bmp
q/rgba32-81284.bmp
    Microsoft specifies no conversion of a bit field to 8 bits, and lib/bmp widens a field by replicating its bits and
    narrows it to its high bits. BMP Suite's references scale each field to the nearest of 255 steps, which it calls
    proper scaling (bmpsuite.html, at q/rgb16-231.bmp), and the two differ by one at some values.

q/pal1huffmsb.bmp
    Modified Huffman 1D compression of OS/2's version 2 header, which lib/bmp says is NotSupported.

q/pal8os2-hs.bmp
    An OS/2 version 2 header that asks for halftoning, a compression that lib/bmp says is NotSupported.

q/rgb24jpeg.bmp
q/rgb24png.bmp
    A DIB that embeds a JPEG or a PNG image, which lib/bmp says is NotSupported.

q/rgb24prof2.bmp
    Its embedded profile describes the pixels; the reference is the picture converted to sRGB, and the comparison
    reads the pixels as stored.

q/rgb24rle24.bmp
    RLE24, OS/2's 24-bit run-length encoding, which lib/bmp says is NotSupported.

q/rgba64.bmp
    64-bit pixels of fixed-point linear channels, which lib/bmp says are NotSupported.
