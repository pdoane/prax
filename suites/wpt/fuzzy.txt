# Fuzzy ranges that override a reftest's own, as a browser's metadata overrides them for its runs. An entry is a line
# of the test and a fuzzy meta's content, which may key one of its references, for each test it covers, then the
# indented reason: why the engine's render differs from the reference by more than the test allows, and why that is not
# an error. An override holds for every backend and pixel ratio.

css/css-backgrounds/subpixel-repeat-no-repeat-mix.html maxDifference=0-24;totalPixels=0-40
    The test draws a 146-by-1 image in tiles of 116.8 by 0.8 px, and the reference in tiles of 117 by 1 px, so both
    minify it and sample its mip levels. Mipmap sampling is anisotropic, and its level comes from the footprint's minor
    axis: the test's footprint is minified along both axes and blends in the second level, while the reference's is
    minified along one and keeps the first. A column and a row of 20 pixels beside the image's edge differ, by up to
    23 levels. A renderer that does not minify from mip levels, or that picks one level for both footprints, draws them
    equal.
css/css-images/gradient/gradient-longer-hue-lch-001.html maxDifference=0-1;totalPixels=0-80
css/css-images/gradient/gradient-longer-hue-lch-002.html maxDifference=0-1;totalPixels=0-80
css/css-images/gradient/gradient-longer-hue-lch-006.html maxDifference=0-1;totalPixels=0-80
    The reference names the midpoint of the test's gradient as a stop, color-mix(in lch longer hue, red, blue), which
    is far outside sRGB. lib/draw holds a stop's color in half floats, as it holds colors throughout, so the stop's
    lightness and chroma move by a fraction of a level on their way back to LCH, and up to two one-level columns of 40
    pixels differ from the test, which names no midpoint. A stop held in floats agrees with the test in every column.
