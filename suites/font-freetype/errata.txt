# Cases in which lib/font is right and FreeType is wrong. An entry lists its cases, a key per line, as
# <check>/<font>, followed by the indented reason: what the font holds and why FreeType loses.

outlines/NewCMMath-Regular.otf
    The font's CFF charstrings place points on half units, as glyph 156 (Ucircumflex) starts at (526.5, 779), which
    fontTools reads too. FreeType's CFF driver gives its unhinted outline in whole units, (526, 779).
